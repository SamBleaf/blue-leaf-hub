## 1. Headline finding

The document is a **reasonably well-built generic template that has not been converted into a site-specific SWMS**, and it is **four years out of review**. It would not survive a SafeWork SA audit in its current form, and — more importantly — it does not tell a carpenter on the Edward St roof what is actually protecting them.

Three defects are severe enough to stop work on their own:

1. **The fall controls are discretionary.** Every fall control in the document is qualified — *"if practicable"*, *"where practicable"*, *"where possible"*, *"when possible"*. A SWMS must state the controls that **will** be used (reg 299(2)(c)–(d)). As written, the decision about whether to use a harness or a work platform is deferred to the worker at the moment of exposure. This is the single most common finding in fall-from-height prosecutions.
2. **No scaffold anywhere in the document.** Not in plant, not in the job steps, not in the controls. For framing, trusses, battens, fascia and cladding on a new build, perimeter scaffold is the primary engineering control. Its absence means the highest-order practicable control has been skipped and the document jumps to PPE.
3. **Fall threshold is internally contradictory and partly superseded.** Page 1 says *"Fall from Heights > 2m"*. Pages 6 and 7 say *"When working above 3m"* and *"Falling from greater then 3 meters"*. SA moved from 3 m to more than 2 m effective **1 July 2026**. The 3 m references are now wrong as a matter of law, and the document disagreed with itself even before that.

## 3. Compliance gaps against reg 299

Reg 299(2) sets out what a SWMS **must** contain. Assessed line by line:

| Reg 299(2) requirement | Status | Evidence / gap |
|---|---|---|
| (a) identify the work that is HRCW | **FAIL** | Page 1 lists all 18 HRCW categories as an unmarked menu. Nothing is ticked, circled or struck out. The document does not identify *which* HRCW applies to Edward St. Same defect on the p.2 "Task/Location Specific Risks" table (24 items, none selected). |
| (b) state the hazards and risks | **PARTIAL** | Hazards are stated but generically. Site-specific hazards for a Norwood corner block — overhead aerial services, neighbouring boundary, footpath, stair void — are absent. |
| (c) describe the control measures | **FAIL** | Controls are conditional, not stated. See §4 below. |
| (d) describe how controls are implemented, monitored and reviewed | **FAIL** | "Implemented" is partly covered. **"Monitored" and "reviewed" are absent from every row.** No pre-start check, no scaffold handover inspection, no verification step, no trigger for revision. |
| (2)(e)/(3) take into account workplace circumstances | **FAIL** | Site address is filled in; nothing else in the body is site-specific. Same text would print for any job. |
| take into account the WHS Management Plan | **NOT ADDRESSED** | No field for the principal contractor's WHSMP. Critical — Blue Leaf is frequently the subcontracted carpenter, so the head builder's WHSMP must be taken into account. |
| readily accessible and understandable | **PARTIAL** | Layout is clear, but PPE is icons-only with no text, ~15 spelling errors, and the risk matrix legend has two identical rows (Low and Very Low). |

**Additional statutory gaps**

- **Reg 300 (compliance with SWMS / stop work).** The stop-work trigger appears only inside the signature declaration on p.13. It should be an operative instruction in the body, not buried in a sign-on block.
- **Reg 302/303 (retention and availability).** The footer says *"This document is uncontrolled when printed or downloaded."* The SWMS must be kept and readily available at the workplace for the duration of the HRCW. Stamping the on-site copy "uncontrolled" actively undermines that duty. Remove or reword.
- **Act ss.47–49 (consultation).** Front page records two names with **no dates**; back page records three names dated 27/6/25. Consultation-in-preparation and sign-on have been conflated. There is no record of the toolbox discussion, no HSR field, and no record of what site-specific conditions were discussed.
- **Act s.46 (multi-PCBU coordination).** Blue Leaf is listed as both Company **and** Principal Contractor. Where a head builder holds PC, the coordination duty and the PC's arrangements must be recorded.

## 4. Control wording — the recurring defect

Every one of these is unusable as a stated control. Direct quotes:

| Quote | Location | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| "If practicable harnesses are to be used" | Roof Truss Installation | Defers the decision to the worker at the point of exposure. |
| "Where practicable harnesses to be used" | Falls from heights | As above. |
| "Harnesses to be used when practicable" | Unsecured beam falling | As above. |
| "Suitable elevated work platform to be used where possible" | Falls from heights | "Possible" is not a standard. Either an EWP is on site or it isn't. |
| "where possible always work from secure working platform" | Loading/fixing bearers | Self-cancelling. |
| "Perimeter edge protection to be fixed from secure safe working platform **as you get to area**" | Loading/fixing bearers | Edge protection installed progressively *as you reach the edge* means the worker is exposed before the control exists. Sequencing error, not just wording. |
| "Set up perimiter railing **when possible**" | p.14 free text | As above. |
| "Team lifts and mechanical lifting devices to be used where possible" | Erect wall frame; Roof Truss | As above. |
| "Appropriate PPE to be worn at all times" | 6+ rows | Which PPE? Unenforceable. |
| "Only walk on joists **when the weather is suitable**" | p.14 free text | Undefined. Needs a stated wind/rain stop-work limit. |

**Fix:** replace with hierarchy-ordered, tickable options. The supervisor ticks only what is actually installed on that site, on that day. See `02_Approved_Control_Module_Register.md`.

## 5. Technical errors

### 5.1 Fall arrest vs fall restraint — incoherent (HIGH)

The document states **"Harnesses are only to be used as a fall restraint"** and then, two rows later, sets out a **rescue plan for a suspended worker** describing suspension intolerance, secondary anchors and lowering.

These are mutually exclusive. Restraint prevents a worker reaching a position from which a fall is possible — there is no suspension to rescue from. If a worker can end up hanging, the system is fall **arrest**, which brings a different set of obligations the document does not meet:

- No anchor point rating specified (single-person anchor rating / AS/NZS 5532; system selection per AS/NZS 1891.4).
- No free-fall distance or **ground clearance calculation**. On a single-storey plate at ~2.4 m, a standard lanyard with a shock absorber cannot arrest a fall before ground contact. This is the most dangerous silent gap in the document.
- No shock absorber requirement, no anchor installation by a competent person.
- No inspection/tagging regime for harnesses and lanyards.
- **No prohibition on anchoring to unbraced trusses.** Roof trusses will not take arrest loads before permanent bracing. This is currently unaddressed and workers could reasonably read "harnesses to be used" as permission.

### 5.2 Rescue plan not viable (HIGH)

The plan says *"Work quickly to prevent suspesion intoerance as you are limited to 5 minutes"* then relies on improvising a secondary anchor and pulley at height, and on calling 000.

- Calling 000 is **not** a rescue plan. Emergency service response time exceeds safe suspension time.
- No nominated trained rescuer on site, no rescue equipment listed, no location for that equipment, no competency record.
- The improvised-pulley method is not a validated technique and would put a second worker at height in an emergency.

**Fix:** if fall arrest is used at all, the rescue method must be specific, equipped, rehearsed and staffed before work starts. Preferable: design fall arrest out — scaffold, EWP or restraint-only with clearance verified.

### 5.3 Three points of contact (MEDIUM)

*"Ensure there is always 3 points of contact ... E.g. 2 hands 1 foot or 1 hand 2 feet, **or harness and 2 feet**."* A harness is not a point of contact. Delete that limb.

### 5.4 Noise controlled solely by PPE (MEDIUM)

The only noise control anywhere is hearing protection. The document's own note says HoC levels 1–5 must be considered before level 6 for work at height — the same logic applies to noise. No consideration of low-noise plant, task separation, or exposure duration. And where PPE is the control, audiometric testing follows.

### 5.5 Dust control wording (MEDIUM)

*"Filtered face mask to be used at all times while cutting material such as wood."* "Filtered face mask" is not a defined class of RPE. Minimum P2, fit-tested. And for anything cement-based, on-tool extraction or wet suppression comes **before** RPE.

### 5.6 Permit table asserts permits you probably don't hold (MEDIUM)

All four permits — Work at Height, Hot Work, Confined Space, Excavation/Penetration — are marked **"Yes"**. This reads as an unedited template default. Asserting a confined space permit applies pulls in the full confined space regime (entry permit, atmospheric monitoring, standby person). Declaring a permit you do not operate is worse than declaring none.

### 5.7 Risk ratings not defensible (MEDIUM)

- **Roof Truss Installation** — falls rated **M**, residual **VL**, HoC applied **6 (PPE only)**. Truss erection is a recognised fatality mechanism. Rating it Medium with PPE-only controls and a Very Low residual will not withstand scrutiny — and it contradicts the document's own instruction on the same page that levels 1–5 must be considered first for all work at height.
- Several rows are rated **H**. The legend says High requires re-planning or *"a separate Hazard Assessment prior to completing the SWMS."* No separate hazard assessments are attached. The document breaches its own procedure.
- Legend rows **Low** and **Very Low** have identical text.

## 7. Structure — HRCW diluted with non-HRCW content

Roughly **half** the document is not high risk construction work: circular saw operation, ladder use, manual handling, UV and heat, housekeeping, nail guns, test-and-tag, cleanup.

These all need controls — but SafeWork SA is explicit that packing a SWMS with non-HRCW content can stop it being effective at communicating the HRCW controls. A carpenter looking for the fall control on page 6 has to read past blade-changing procedure and sunscreen timing.

This is exactly the split the three-part pack is designed to fix. Assignments are set out in `03_Base_Pack_Template.md`.

## 9. What was kept, changed, rejected

### KEPT (carried into the base template)
- Overall table architecture: Job Step → Hazard → Risk Rating → Control → HoC → Residual → Responsible. Sound skeleton.
- 5×5 risk matrix and the six-level hierarchy of control.
- The note *"HoC levels 1–5 must be considered before level 6 for all Work at Height activities."* Good rule — the document just doesn't follow it.
- Rescue/Retrieval Planning trigger table (p.12). Good concept, weak execution.
- Worker declaration wording on the sign-on page (stop-work obligation). Strong; needs promoting into the body.
- Ladder angle 1:4. Correct.
- AS/NZS 3012 and AS/NZS 3760 references for site electrical. Correct standards.
- Emergency resources block. Needs real entries, not "refer to evacuation plan".

### CHANGED
- All conditional control wording → hierarchy-ordered selectable options with a tick box.
- All "3 m" → ">2 m".
- HRCW menu → **selection** field that identifies which categories apply to this job.
- "Person Responsible" → split into *installs/verifies* (named supervisor) and *uses* (worker).
- Reg 299(2)(d) → new explicit **Monitoring & Review** column on every module.
- Non-HRCW content → moved to Part 2 task-control modules.
- PPE icons → text-based conditional PPE matrix with required / conditional / recommended flags.
- Rescue plan → equipment, nominated rescuer and method stated before work starts.

### REJECTED (do not carry forward)
- "If/where practicable" and "where possible" phrasing in any control. Banned.
- "Harnesses are only to be used as a fall restraint" alongside a suspension rescue plan. Incoherent — pick one and specify it properly.
- "harness and 2 feet" as three points of contact.
- Blanket "Yes" on all four permits.
- "MSDS Required: nil".
- "Plant & Equipment: Standard tools".
- "This document is uncontrolled when printed or downloaded" on a SWMS.
- Unstructured free text after the signature page.
