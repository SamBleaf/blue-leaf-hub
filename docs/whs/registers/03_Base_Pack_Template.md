## Pack rules (print on the cover of every pack)

1. **Part 1 is the SWMS.** It contains high risk construction work only. Nothing else goes in it.
2. **Part 2 is not the SWMS.** It contains important non-HRCW controls and is physically separated by a divider page.
3. Every control in Parts 1 and 2 is a **selection**, not a description. If a control is not ticked, it is not in place, and the work it protects does not proceed.
4. **No control may contain conditional wording** — *if practicable, where possible, when practicable, as required, appropriate PPE*. If it can't be stated definitively, it isn't a control.
5. Nothing is issued until **Part 3 §3.9 is signed** by the supervisor and every worker.
6. Any material change → **new version → re-consultation → re-signature**.
7. **The site copy is the controlled copy.** It stays at the location of the HRCW for the duration of the work.

**Rules for this table**
- The **Install / verify** column names a **person**, not "Worker". Blanket "Worker" on installed controls is not accountability.
- **How monitored** and **Review trigger** are mandatory on every row — this is reg 299(2)(d) and it was absent from the superseded document.
- Levels 1–5 must be considered before level 6 for every work-at-height row. A row where the only selected control is level 6 requires written justification in §3.8.
- Any row rated **High** requires re-planning or a separate hazard assessment attached to the pack before issue.
