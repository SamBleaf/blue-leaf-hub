# WHS Outputs Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix is the rulebook. The risk engine should consume this matrix logic. Rules must not be scattered through UI code.

| Trigger | SWMS | Permit | Inspection | Register | Toolbox | Site Board Warning | Training/Competency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Work at heights >2m | Working at Heights | Height work permit per policy | Fall protection/harness check | Risk Register | Falls | Fall risk, protection required | Heights awareness |
| Roof work | Roof Work | None unless internal policy | Roof access/edge check | Risk Register | Roof safety | Roof work in progress | Roof work competency |
| Scaffold use | Scaffold Use | None unless internal policy | Handover and periodic check | Scaffold Register | Scaffold use | Do not alter scaffold | Scaffolder ticket if required |
| Excavation/trenching | Excavation | Excavation permit where required | Daily excavation inspection | Risk Register | Excavation | Open excavation | Excavation awareness |
| Excavation >1.5m / near services / near structures | Excavation + shoring controls | Excavation Permit + DBYD evidence | Daily and after rain | Risk + Inspection Register | Trench safety | Deep excavation, no unauthorised entry | Trench/shoring competency |
| Demolition | Demolition | Demolition permit/notification where applicable | Pre-demo hazard check | Risk Register | Demolition | Demolition zone | Demolition awareness |
| Structural alteration / temporary support | Structural Carpentry | None unless internal policy | Temporary support check | Risk Register | Structural work | Temporary supports in place | Competent person |
| Crane lifts | Crane/Lifting | Lift plan / permit where required | Pre-lift check | Plant Register | Crane lift | Lifting exclusion zone | Crane operator/dogger/rigger |
| Telehandler/mobile plant | Mobile Plant | None unless internal policy | Daily pre-start | Plant Register | Mobile plant | Plant exclusion zone | Operator competency |
| Hot works | Hot Works | Hot Work Permit | Fire watch and post-work check | Risk Register | Hot works | Hot works, fire watch active | Hot work awareness |
| Silica cutting/grinding/chasing/core drilling | Silica/RCS | None unless internal policy | Dust control/RPE check | Risk + Health Monitoring flag | Silica dust | RPE required | Silica awareness |
| Hazardous chemicals | Hazardous Chemicals | None unless internal policy | SDS/storage check | SDS Register | Chemical handling | Hazardous chemicals stored here | Chemical handling |
| Confined space | Confined Space | Confined Space Entry Permit | Atmospheric test + standby | Risk Register | Confined space | Permit only | Confined-space competency |
| Suspended slab/formwork | Formwork/Falsework | None unless internal policy | Pre-pour formwork inspection | Risk Register | Formwork | Loaded formwork, keep clear | Formwork competency |
| Energised electrical | Electrical Work | Isolation permit | Test-for-dead/isolation check | Risk Register | Electrical safety | Live electrical work | Licensed electrician |
| Asbestos risk | Asbestos | Removal control plan where required | Clearance inspection | Asbestos Register | Asbestos awareness | Do not disturb | Licensed removalist where required |
| Public interface | Traffic/Public Protection | Council/road permit where required | Barricade/signage check | Controls Register | Public protection | No public entry | Traffic control if required |
| Overhead powerlines | Powerline Controls | Authority clearance if required | Clearance check | Controls Register | Powerline safety | Overhead powerlines | Spotter/plant competency |
| Underground services | Service Location Controls | DBYD evidence | Service location verification | Controls Register | Service strike prevention | Locate before dig | Excavation awareness |
