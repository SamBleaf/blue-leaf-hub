## 5. Site rules

{{site_rules}}

Core site rules:

- Complete site induction before starting.
- Sign in and out.
- Keep site secure.
- Keep work areas clean.
- Use correct PPE.
- Follow SWMS for high risk work.
- Do not alter scaffold, edge protection or temporary supports.
- Do not enter exclusion zones.
- Report hazards and incidents immediately.
- No unauthorised visitors.
- No drugs or alcohol onsite.

## 8. Public and neighbour controls

{{public_interface_controls}}

Minimum controls:

- maintain fencing and signage
- keep footpaths and roads clear unless approved controls are in place
- control dust, noise and waste
- secure materials
- supervise deliveries
- protect neighbouring property

## 9. Daily supervisor checks

The site supervisor must check:

- site access is safe
- fencing/signage is in place
- emergency equipment is accessible
- amenities are usable
- housekeeping is acceptable
- exclusion zones are maintained
- high risk work has current SWMS
- required permits/inspections are complete
- new hazards are recorded

## 10. Worker acknowledgement

Workers acknowledge site rules through the induction and relevant SWMS sign-on.
