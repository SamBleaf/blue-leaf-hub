## 3. Duty holders and responsibilities

### Principal contractor

{{principal_contractor}} is responsible for coordinating WHS arrangements for the construction project so far as reasonably practicable.

Responsibilities include:

- ensuring site risks are identified and controlled
- ensuring site-specific WHS documents are prepared and available
- coordinating subcontractor WHS requirements
- ensuring high risk construction work is not carried out unless an applicable SWMS is in place
- maintaining site induction, SWMS, incident, inspection and consultation records
- reviewing controls when site conditions or work methods change

### Site supervisor

{{site_supervisor_name}} is responsible for day-to-day site coordination.

Contact: {{site_supervisor_phone}}

Responsibilities include:

- enforcing site rules
- confirming workers and subcontractors are inducted
- confirming SWMS are available, signed and followed
- stopping unsafe work
- recording incidents, hazards and corrective actions
- arranging toolbox talks and consultation
- checking site controls remain effective

### Workers and subcontractors

All workers and subcontractors must:

- complete site induction before starting work
- follow site rules and relevant SWMS
- use required PPE
- report hazards, incidents and near misses
- stop work if unsafe conditions arise
- only carry out work they are competent and authorised to perform

## 4. WHS consultation and communication

Consultation will be managed through:

- site inductions
- toolbox talks
- SWMS briefings and sign-ons
- worker hazard reports
- safety alerts
- site meetings where required
- consultation records in the WHS consultation register

Workers must be given a reasonable opportunity to raise WHS concerns and contribute to control measures before and during relevant work.

## 9. Risk management process

Blue Leaf Building will manage risk by:

1. Identifying hazards
2. Assessing risk
3. Applying controls using the hierarchy of control
4. Communicating controls to affected workers
5. Monitoring controls
6. Reviewing controls when conditions change

Controls must be reviewed when:

- an incident or near miss occurs
- a hazard is reported
- site conditions change
- work methods change
- a SWMS is not being followed
- new plant, equipment, substances or subcontractors are introduced

## 15. Hazardous substances and SDS

Hazardous chemicals must not be brought onsite unless:

- the SDS is available
- storage requirements are understood
- PPE is available
- workers are trained or instructed in safe use
- spills and disposal controls are in place

## 17. Incident and hazard reporting

All incidents, injuries, near misses and hazards must be reported to the site supervisor immediately.

Records must be entered into the incident/hazard reporting system.

Notifiable incidents must be escalated immediately and the incident scene preserved where required.

## 18. Corrective actions

Corrective actions must include:

- description of the issue
- required action
- responsible person
- due date
- completion date
- verification

Open corrective actions must be visible on the project WHS dashboard.

## 19. Review

This plan must be reviewed:

- before construction starts
- after a material project change
- after a serious incident or near miss
- when high risk work changes
- when site conditions change
- at scheduled review intervals
