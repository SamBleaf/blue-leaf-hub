- other fall risk work

## Emergency process

1. Stop work and raise the alarm.
2. Call 000 if injury, suspension trauma risk or rescue cannot be immediate.
3. Notify the site supervisor.
4. Prevent others from entering the danger area.
5. Use planned rescue method only if trained and safe.
6. Provide first aid after rescue.
7. Record and investigate the incident.
