## 7. Emergency response process

### Injury or medical emergency

1. Stop work.
2. Call 000 if serious or life-threatening.
3. Notify the site supervisor.
4. Provide first aid only if trained and safe to do so.
5. Keep access clear for emergency services.
6. Record the incident.
7. Preserve the scene if notifiable and safe to do so.

### Fire

1. Raise the alarm.
2. Evacuate to the assembly point.
3. Call 000.
4. Only use fire equipment if trained and safe to do so.
5. Do not re-enter the work area until cleared.

### Service strike

1. Stop work immediately.
2. Move workers away from the affected area.
3. Do not touch damaged services.
4. Contact emergency services or service provider where required.
5. Isolate the area.
6. Notify the site supervisor.
7. Record the incident.

### Structural collapse or excavation collapse

1. Stop work.
2. Do not enter the collapse zone.
3. Call 000 if anyone is injured, trapped or at risk.
4. Establish exclusion zone.
5. Keep plant and loads away from the area.
6. Wait for emergency services or competent advice.

### Fall from height

1. Stop work.
2. Call 000 if serious.
3. Do not move the injured person unless required to protect life.
4. Implement rescue plan if suspended in harness.
5. Notify the site supervisor.
6. Record and investigate.

## 10. Emergency review

Emergency arrangements must be reviewed:
