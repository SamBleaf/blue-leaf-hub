# SOP: Capture the project brief — The project, Rooms, Throughout, Site & existing

**Module:** Sales → Lead → Project brief
**SOP ID:** 21-01
**Status:** Draft
**Priority:** High

---

## 1. Who uses this
Director, sales lead, estimator and project manager: anyone meeting the client, walking the site or going through the plans with them. Office staff use the same screens to tidy up after a meeting.

## 2. When to use it
- At a Discovery meeting, a concept meeting or a site walk.
- When you brief the architect at the Consultants stage.
- Any time the client tells you what they want.

Detailed room work normally starts once the PTSA is signed, but you can work ahead at any stage. Nothing waits for a payment.

## 3. What this does
It records the client's story, the site, the design team and every room in one place, in the client's words. It saves as you go. The **architect's brief**, the **client brief** and the **selections schedule** are all made from what you record here, so nothing is typed twice. The client's story and budget are saved straight to the client's details, so Sales shows the same. Each room has one **Work** word (New, Renovated, Altered, No change), and that word is the only description of the room's work that prints. A short **room brief** says what the room must do. The screens show the gaps the architect's brief still has, and where to fix each one.

## 4. Before you start
- The lead exists and has a project type (Sales → the lead → its details).
- You are signed in as staff. Clients on the portal never see this screen.
- Know which drawings you are working from, if there are any (for example "DA rev 3").
- The register must be switched on for this lead. While it is a pilot, a lead that isn't in the pilot shows the old **Finishes schedule** list instead. Ask the director to add the lead to the pilot.
- On an iPad or phone, have a reasonable signal. If you lose signal, your answers are kept on the device and saved when it comes back (see 5.6).

## 5. Step-by-step process

> 💡 **If you used the old screens:** "Project" is now **The project**. "Whole house" is now **Throughout**. The room table moved from Project to **Rooms**. "From Whole House" in a room is now **Same as the rest of the house**, and "Confirm room defaults" is now **Confirm all**. "Office fields" is now **Product details…** in each row's ⋯ menu. "Save & next room" is now **Next: (room name) ›**. "Existing — Remain" is now **No change**, and "Not in scope" is now **Not in this job**. The **Show detailed prompts** tick is gone: detailed questions are always under **More choices**.

### 5.1 Open the brief

1. Go to **Sales → Pipeline** and open the lead.
2. In the stage panel, find the card headed **Project brief**. The grey line under the heading names the project type, suburb, drawings and number of rooms, for example "Extension · Seaview Park · Drawings DA rev 3 · 16 rooms".
3. If the card is closed, tap **Start brief →** (nothing recorded yet) or **Continue brief →**. It reopens where you last stopped.
4. Read the header, left to right:
   - the save state: **Saving…**, **Saved 9:35 pm** (green) or **Not saved — retrying** (red);
   - **Save**, which saves now;
   - **Actions 10**, the number of points waiting on someone. An amber dot means some of them have no one named yet. Tap it to open the list;
   - **⤢ Expand** (tablet and desktop) opens the brief full width. Tap **Exit full width ✕** to leave;
   - **⋯** holds **Ask about the existing building**. It is ticked and greyed for an Extension or Renovation. For any other project type, tick it to ask the existing-building questions;
   - **Collapse** closes the card.
5. Read the tabs: **The project · Rooms · Throughout · Site & existing · Documents**, then a divider and the office tabs **Register · Tender** in grey. **Tender** appears from the Consultants stage. **Handover** appears at the Won stage, and at any stage once the contract is signed. On a phone the row of tabs scrolls sideways.

### 5.2 The project

**The gaps strip (top)**

1. Read the amber strip, for example "Still missing for the architect's brief: the client's vision · must-haves · budget · the architect's name" and "4 gaps" on the right. It only shows while something is missing. On a phone it is one line, "4 gaps for the architect's brief ›". Tap it to see the list.
2. Tap a gap. The screen jumps to that field and puts the cursor in it.
3. Fill it in. The gap leaves the strip as soon as it saves.

A missing vision, must-haves or budget only warns. The architect's brief can still be issued without them. A missing drawing set does stop the brief being issued (see Drawings below).

**The identity line**

4. Check the grey line: client · site address · project type · delivery model · PTSA status.
5. To change the delivery model, tap it (it has a dotted underline). Choose **Design & build**, **Build only**, **Construction management** or **Other**.
6. If the project type isn't set yet, choose it from **Set project type…**.
7. To change anything else on this line, tap **Change in Sales ›**. It takes you to the lead's own details. Change it there, not here.

**The client's story** (saved to the client's details — Sales shows the same)

8. **What they want, in their words.** Type it the way the client said it. While it's empty it reads "Not recorded — the architect's brief opens with this."
9. **Must-haves.** One per line: what the design can't do without.
10. **What worries them.** Their biggest concern about the build (optional).
11. **Top priorities (pick up to 3).** Tap up to three chips. If words were typed here before, a line reads "Noted earlier: Natural light, Space". Tap **Use these** to tick the chips those words name. Words that match no chip stay in the note.
12. Each box saves about a second after you stop typing, or when you leave it. You will see **Saving…** then **Saved ✓**. If a save fails you will see **Not saved — try again**. Tap **try again**. Your words stay in the box.

**Budget and timing**

13. **Construction budget (ex GST).** Tap the band the client gives: **Under $400k · $400k–$600k · $600k–$800k · $800k–$1m · $1m–$1.5m · Over $1.5m**. Tap the same band again to clear it.
14. For an exact figure, tap **Exact…** and type **$ From** and **$ To** (850,000 or 850k both work). A range that isn't one of the bands shows as a chosen chip, for example "Exact: $850,000 – $950,000".
15. Tick **Confirmed with the client** only when the client has confirmed the figure. **The band prints in the architect's brief only once this is ticked.** Until then the brief says "To be confirmed by Blue Leaf." If someone typed other words in Sales (for example "Pending bank approval"), they show as "Noted in Sales: …". You are asked before the toggle changes them.
16. **Paying by.** Tap **Cash**, **Need finance**, **Pre-approved** or **Unsure**. Tap it again to clear it.
17. **Target start.** Choose the month, then the year. Nothing saves until both are chosen.
18. Optional: tap **+ What they're most excited about** and type it.

On a phone, What worries them, the priorities, Confirmed, Paying by and the excited line sit behind **More about the client (n) ▸**.

**Site and planning** (from public mapping — a guide only)

19. Read the rows: **Council**, **Planning zone**, **Bushfire**, **Slope**, **Site complexity**, **Access**. You can't edit them here. Bushfire reads "In a mapped bushfire area" with "BAL to be assessed", or "Not in a mapped bushfire area", or "Not confirmed" when public mapping has no answer. It never reads "No" just because nothing is known.
20. To look the site up again, tap **Re-check**. You will see "Checking public mapping…", then "Updated 17 Nov". If the lookup can't run you will see a plain message, for example "Couldn't reach the mapping service. Nothing changed." or "Checked less than 10 minutes ago, so it wasn't run again." Nothing you already have is lost. If you see "Couldn't find the site address on the map. Check the address in Sales.", correct the site address or suburb in Sales, then tap **Re-check** again: it runs straight away, with no wait. (Re-typing the same address still waits a minute.)
21. The **Access** value is a link (for example "Standard driveway"). Tap it to open that answer in a side panel and change it.
22. **During the works.** Tap **Moving out**, **Partly lived in** or **Living there**. It saves on the tap.
23. If an older note about living on site exists, it shows as "Earlier note: “…”". Tap **Tidy up** to reword it or remove it.

On a phone the site rows shrink to one line, for example "City of Seaview · Hills Neighbourhood · Bushfire area · High complexity ›". Tap it to see them all. **During the works** stays on screen.

**Design team**

24. Read the rows: **Architect**, **Engineer**, **Energy assessor**, then anyone else. "Not named" in amber on the Architect row means the brief will be addressed to "The design team".
25. Tap **Name the architect**. A panel opens with a search box, **Search people and firms**.
26. Type two or more letters. Rows show "Name · Firm · role". Someone already on this job says **On this job** and can't be picked twice.
27. Tap the person. They are saved at once and the panel closes.
28. If they aren't in the contacts, tap **+ Add someone new**. Fill in **Name (required)**, **Firm**, **Email** and **Phone**, then tap **Save**. Tap **Back to search** to go back.
29. If someone else already has that email, you will see "Someone else uses that email: … Use them, or change the email." Tap **Use (their name)** if it is the same person, or **Back** to change the email. Nobody is linked without you choosing.
30. The Architect row now reads "Name — Firm" with a ⋯ menu: **Change** or **Remove from this job**. Removing asks first, and the person stays in the client's contacts.
31. Use **+ Add** on the Engineer and Energy assessor rows, and **+ Another consultant** for a land surveyor, soil and geotech, interior designer, lighting, sanitary and tapware, or another consultant.

If a different architect is typed outside the register (the Fee Proposal wizard's Architect box, or an RFQ Engine extraction), the Hub asks "Change it everywhere?". **OK** replaces the architect on the design team (and the job); **Cancel** leaves the design team as it is. The architect is kept in one place: the design team. See 21-03 TC-21.

If an architect's name was typed in Sales before, it shows under the row as "Noted in Sales: “…”", and **Name the architect** starts its search with that name. An older design-team note shows as "Noted earlier: “…”" with **Edit**. On a phone only the Architect row shows; tap **Engineer, energy assessor and others ›** for the rest.

**Drawings**

32. In **Drawings this brief is based on**, type the drawing set and revision (for example "DA rev 3"). While it's empty it reads "Not recorded — the brief can't be issued without it."
33. The line under it says how many drawing files Blue Leaf has. Tap **Upload in Files ›** to add one in the lead's Files.

**Who does what** (Build only, Construction management or Other)

34. Tap **Who does what (construction management) ▸** to open the questions about who manages what. It is closed by default. It also stays when the delivery model changes but an answer is already recorded there.

### 5.3 Rooms — the list

1. Tap **Rooms**. The list opens, grouped by floor in walk order, for example "GROUND FLOOR · 16".
2. Read the summary under the title, for example "8 new · 8 renovated".
3. Read each row:
   - line 1: the room's name, its **Work** pill, an action pill if anything is waiting (for example "1 action"), and ⋯;
   - line 2: the room brief, or an amber dot and **Brief needed** followed by its must-haves in grey;
   - amber tags at the end: **Check work** (an earlier answer disagrees with the Work word) or **Unfinished: “…”** (the brief stops mid-sentence).
4. Use the filter to narrow the list: **All 16 · Brief needed 15 · Check work 3**. On a phone it reads **Brief** and **Check**. The filter is remembered on this device.

**Add rooms**

5. Tap **+ Add room** (on a phone, the round **+**). Once the job has two or more floors, each floor header has its own **+ Add room here**.
6. The panel reads "Add to Ground floor as". Tap the Work word once: **New**, **Renovated**, **Altered** or **No change**. Nothing is chosen for you the first time. Until you choose, the room chips are greyed and the panel says "Choose the work first: each room you tap is added with it."
7. Tap a room chip: Entry · Living · Dining · Kitchen · Pantry · Scullery · Laundry · Bathroom · Ensuite · WC · Bedroom · Robe · Study · Stairs · Garage · Alfresco · Outside. The room is added at the end of that floor and numbered for you ("Bedroom" becomes "Bedroom 5"). A message says "Added Bedroom 5 on the ground floor" with **Undo**.
8. Keep tapping chips: each tap adds one more room. The panel stays open.
9. For a room that isn't a chip, tap **Other…**, type its name (for example "Rumpus") and tap **Add**.
10. Tap **Done**. The Work word you chose is remembered until you close the browser tab.

**Add a floor**

11. Tap **+ Add a floor**. Tap **Upper floor** or **Lower ground**, or type a short name (for example "Loft") and tap **Add floor**.
12. The add panel opens on the new floor. Until a room is on it, the floor header offers **Remove floor**, and once you tap **Done** the floor shows "No rooms on this floor yet."

**Move rooms upstairs in one go** (tick mode)

13. If every room is on one floor but the brief mentions an upper level or stairs, an amber box asks "Is anything upstairs?" and says why, for example "Every room is on the ground floor, but the cladding is recorded for the upper level and the living room mentions stairs."
14. Tap **Choose upstairs rooms**. The title becomes "Tick the rooms that are upstairs". Nothing is ticked for you, and the pills hide.
15. Tap each room that is upstairs. The button at the bottom counts them, for example "Move 5 rooms to Upper floor".
16. Tap that button. All of them move in one save. A message says "Moved 5 rooms to the upper floor" with **Undo** for about 10 seconds. The list now has "GROUND FLOOR · 11" and "UPPER FLOOR · 5", and the amber box goes.
17. To stop without moving anything, tap **Cancel**. If you had ticked rooms, it asks "Discard 3 floor changes?". Tap **Discard** or **Keep ticking**.
18. **Not now** hides the amber box for this job on this device. The Documents tab still warns about it.

**Change a room**

19. Tap a room's **Work** pill and choose **New**, **Renovated**, **Altered**, **No change** or (under the line) **Not in this job**. It saves on the tap. If an earlier answer now disagrees, the row gains **Check work**.
20. Tap ⋯ on a row for: **Rename · Duplicate · Move to another floor · Move up · Move down · Room type and drawing sheet… · Remove room…**
    - **Duplicate** copies the room's type, floor and Work word only, never its answers. It says "Added Bedroom 5 below Bedroom 4 — its answers weren't copied".
    - **Room type** sets which choices the room asks. Nothing already recorded is lost. **Drawing sheet** takes a sheet number such as "A102 rev C".
    - **Remove room…** asks first and names what goes with it, for example "Remove Laundry and its 6 answers? Anything already on the client portal is kept." Tap **Remove** or **Cancel**.
    - A room holding something on the client portal or in the contract can't be removed. Its menu says "Can't remove: it holds items on the client portal or in the contract" and offers **Not in this job** instead.
21. On a desktop you can also drag a row to reorder it, or drop it on another floor's header.
22. Tap anywhere else on a row to open that room.

### 5.4 A room (tablet and desktop)

The room is one column, top to bottom.

**1 The room bar** (stays at the top)

1. Read it: **‹ Rooms** on the left, the room's name over "Ground floor ▾ · Room 4 of 16", and the save state on the right ("Saved 9:41 pm", "Saving…" or "Not saved — will retry"). On a room the save state shows here, not in the header.
2. To change the floor, tap **Ground floor ▾** and tap another floor chip, or **+ Floor** to name a new one.

**2 Work**

3. Tap the room's Work word: **New · Renovated · Altered · No change**. It saves on the tap.
4. The ⋯ beside it holds **Not in this job · Room details (name, type, drawing sheet) · Duplicate room · Remove room…**
   - **No change**: the brief and choices fold to one line, "No work planned here — no brief needed."
   - **Not in this job**: a message says "(Room) is marked not part of this job." with **Undo**. The room then shows only "This room isn't part of the job." and **Bring it back**.

**3 An earlier answer that disagrees**

5. If an older answer about the whole room disagrees with the Work word, an amber box says so, for example "An earlier answer says “New kitchen”, but this room is set to Renovated. Only one can print."
6. Tap **Keep Renovated** to keep the room's word. The earlier answer is removed, and a message says "Removed “New kitchen”." with **Undo**.
7. Or tap **Change to New**. The room becomes New and any earlier answer that now disagrees is removed, with the same **Undo**.
8. If another earlier answer still disagrees, the box shows that one next.
9. After the contract is signed the box says "This room is part of the signed contract — change it through a variation." and offers no buttons.

**4 Room brief** (first, with a blue edge)

10. Read the caption on the right: "In the architect's brief" (grey), or "Needed before the architect's brief can go" (amber) when the room has work and no brief yet.
11. In **What must this room do?**, type who uses it, what happens here, and what it must be near or keep. The client's words are fine. Press Return (or **Done** on the iPad keyboard) to finish.
12. For a small room with nothing special (a WC, a robe), tap **Nothing special — standard room** under the empty box. It writes, for example, "Standard WC; no special requirements." and counts as the room's brief.
13. If the sentence ends in a dash or a colon, you will see "Looks unfinished — it won't print until it's finished." Finish the sentence.
14. **Connects to.** Tap the rooms this room opens onto, or **Outside**. The nearest rooms show first; tap **+ More rooms** for the rest. The other room then shows, in grey, "Connects to Kitchen (set from the Kitchen)". Nothing is copied.
15. **Must-haves.** Tap the chips for this room type (for example **Layout & family use** and **Storage** in a kitchen). If words were typed before, "Noted earlier: Full-height, Walk-in, Drawers" shows with **Use these**. Tap it to tick the matching chips.
16. Tap **+ Who uses it · Rough size** if you know them. Tap **Whole family**, **Adults**, **Children**, **Guests** or **Working from home**, and type a rough size in m².
17. Tap **How this room reads in the architect's brief ▸** to see the room's printed row: name, Work word and text. Anything held back says why, for example "Not printed: the earlier answer “New kitchen” (disagrees with the room)."

**5 Choices for this room**

18. Each choice is one row: its name, a grey or amber status line, its chips, and on the right **⚑** and **⋯**. Press and hold (or right-click) a row's name to read the full question; tap **Hide** to close it.
19. Tap the chips the client chooses. A chosen chip shows a tick. Only the next level of choices appears after a tap.
20. Tap **More choices (n)** for the rest. **Office only (n)**, closed, sits at the end of that list.
21. Tap ⋯ on a row for exactly these:
    - **Not decided yet**: the row says "Not decided yet" and the ⚑ turns amber;
    - **Not needed here**;
    - **Note for the office (doesn't print)**: a short staff-only note;
    - **Product details…**: product, manufacturer, model, colour, supplier, client wording, supplied by, technical check, drawing reference, approvals and the client portal;
    - **Remove answer**: asks "Remove this answer in Kitchen? It comes off the register." It only shows once the row has an answer.

    On a record fixed by the signed contract only **Note for the office** and **Product details…** are offered.
22. **Appliances — who supplies?**: set **Mostly supplied by** (**Blue Leaf**, **Client** or **Others**) first, then tap each appliance. Tap a chosen appliance to change who supplies it, flag it, or **Remove** it.
23. The engineered-stone benchtop is never offered. If an older answer holds it, it shows as "Engineered stone · not allowed" and the row says to choose another with the client.
24. On a renovation, **Existing-to-new junctions & make good** shows the work recorded for this room on Site & existing ("The same record as Site & existing: a change here shows there."). Tap **+ Existing work here** to add one.
25. If an earlier answer changed and something chosen under it no longer fits, a box lists it under "Chosen before an earlier answer changed:". Tap **Use again** or **Remove**. Nothing disappears by itself.

**⚑ Needs action from…** (on any row)

26. Tap **⚑**. A panel opens with **Who**, **By when** and **What's needed**.
27. If **What's needed** is empty, type it first (for example "Confirm the island clearance").
28. Tap who it's waiting on: **Client**, **Architect**, **Engineer**, **Energy assessor**, **Blue Leaf** or **Trade or supplier**. When the Hub knows who usually answers this question, that chip is marked "suggested" (some rows, such as the washer / dryer layout, have no suggestion). Once a firm is on the design team, its chip names them.
29. Check **By when**: **Next drawing issue**, **Next meeting** or **Pick a date…**. It is set from the suggested chip, or from the chip you tap if there was no suggestion.
30. Close the panel. The ⚑ is now a pill, for example "Architect · Next issue". Tap it to change it, or tap **Done** (or **Checked** for a technical check) or **Clear**.

Blue Leaf's own follow-ups never print in the architect's brief. They stay in the Actions list until they are done.

**6 Same as the rest of the house**

31. Read the grey strip: a summary of the house choices this room takes, for example "Floor: timber, engineered · Walls: warm · Ceiling: square set, flat · Cabinetry: flat-panel, laminate".
32. If it says **Confirm all (4)**, check the summary with the client and tap it. It then reads "✓ Confirmed 17 Nov". This only records that the house choices apply in this room. It isn't the client's approval, a technical check or a price.
33. If one line is different in this room, tap **Different here**. Each line shows its status (**To confirm**, **Confirmed**, **Changed since confirmed** or **Different here**). Tap **Different here** on that line and choose. Other rooms don't change.
34. House choices still open for this room show under the strip: red when they can't be used (for example "Benchtop: engineered stone can't be used — choose another with the client" with **Change**), amber when still to decide (for example "Splashback: tiles allowed, client to decide" with **Open**). **Change** and **Open** open the whole-house choice in a side panel. A change there changes it for the whole house.

**7 Actions in this room**

35. Read the list of what's waiting on someone in this room, or "Nothing waiting on anyone here."
36. To add one, tap **+ Needs action from…**, type what's needed and tap who.
37. Tap **✓** on an action that's done. It says "Marked done." with **Undo**. A technical check asks for the **Drawing or document reference** (for example "A301 Rev D detail 4") before it closes as "Marked checked."

**8 Other things recorded here (n) ▸**

38. This shows only when the room holds something no row above shows (for example "Earlier answer: Renovate — agrees with the room"). Tap ⋯ on one for **Show details** or **Remove** (with Undo). Something on the client portal or in the contract can't be removed from here.

**9 Moving on**

39. Tap **Next: (room name) ›** at the bottom right, or **‹ (room name)** at the bottom left. The last room says **Done — back to Rooms**.
40. The brief saves first. The button shows **Saving…**. If it can't save within about 8 seconds you stay on the room and see "Not saved yet — check your connection. Your answers are kept." Try again once the connection is back.

### 5.5 A room on a phone

1. The room bar reads "‹" (back to Rooms), the name over "Ground floor · 4 of 16 · Saved", and ⋯.
2. ⋯ holds **‹ Previous room: (name)**, **Floor: (floor)**, **Not in this job**, **Room details**, **Duplicate room** and **Remove room…**. There is no swiping between rooms, so you can't change room by accident.
3. Work is a full-width row. The earlier-answer buttons stack.
4. Menus, the ⚑ panel, Product details and the house lines open as panels from the bottom of the screen. Tap outside, or ✕, to close them.
5. The house strip is one row, for example "Same as the rest of the house … ✓ 4 confirmed ›" or "Confirm 4 ›". Tap it for the lines, **Confirm all** and **Different here**. The red and amber lines stay under the strip, shortened (for example "Splashback: client to decide").
6. The big **Next: (room name) ›** button sits above the Hub's bottom bar. It hides while you type and comes back when you finish.
7. The lead's stage bar is hidden while a room is open (and while you tick rooms upstairs), so two bars never stack. On the capture tabs the lead's header scrolls away to give you room.

### 5.6 No signal or a weak signal

1. Keep tapping. Chips change on the screen straight away.
2. The room bar reads **Not saved — will retry** in amber. (While a room is open the header shows no save state; on the other tabs the header reads **Not saved — retrying**.) The brief keeps trying by itself.
3. If you tap **Next** on a phone, it waits up to about 8 seconds, then says "Still not saved. Stay here or carry on — your answers are kept on this phone."
4. Tap **Stay** to wait for signal, or **Carry on** to go to the next room. Your answers are kept and saved when the signal comes back.
5. If the page closes before it saves (a reload, a closed tab, an iPad dropping the page), the brief says "Unsaved changes from (time) are kept on this device" when you come back. Tap **Restore** to save them, or **Discard** to drop them. Anything someone else changed since keeps their newer value, and you're told how many. Signing out clears these copies, and they expire after 7 days.

### 5.7 Throughout (the house-wide choices)

1. Tap **Throughout**. Read the line at the top: "Set the house-wide choices once, starting with what the client wants the home to be. Every room reads these; a room only says so where it's different."
2. Work through the cards: **Envelope & external materials · Internal standards (defaults) · Kitchen & joinery standards · Wet-area standards · Laundry standards · Services & performance · Inclusions & responsibilities**. A card with nothing to ask on this job is left out.
3. The rows work exactly like a room's choices: chips, ⚑, ⋯ (**Not decided yet · Not needed here · Note for the office (doesn't print) · Product details… · Remove answer**), **More choices (n)** and **Office only (n)**.
4. Where each area has its own choice (floors, paint, kitchen, wet areas), every usual area already has a row, so you just tap. Rarer areas are one tap away, for example **+ Scullery**, **+ Ensuite** or **+ Powder / WC**.
5. Where there can be more than one answer (several wall systems, several roofs), tap **+ Add another**.
6. **Roof:** once a roof has an answer, a **Gutters** row appears under it. Gutters are their own choice.
7. **Envelope quality:** an amber **For technical review** box (marked **Review required** and **Staff only**) lists what the chosen goals may prompt someone to check. Nothing in it is selected, specified or priced.
8. At the end, **Blue Leaf pricing guide** (**Staff only**) is for the estimator. Tap **Standard**, **Upgraded** or **Premium** as the project guide, or open **By category**. It never changes what the client chose, and it isn't an approval or a price.

### 5.8 Site & existing

1. Tap **Site & existing**. Read the line at the top: "What the site and the existing house mean for the work. Council, zone and bushfire are on The project."
2. Work through the cards: **Access & occupation · Existing building & interfaces · External works · Surveys, approvals & consultants · Quality priorities**, using the same rows as Throughout.
3. A site fact such as a steep driveway is recorded as a fact, not as building work.
4. **Areas in the project:** type an area (for example "rear extension" or "front garden") and tap whether it's in this job: **Included**, **Optional**, **Excluded** or **Deferred**. Tap **+ Area** for the next one, or **Remove**.
5. **Existing works:** choose the room under **Where**, then what, then what happens to it. It's the same record the room shows. Tap **+ New-to-existing connection** when the new work joins the existing house.
6. Who lives on site during the works is now **During the works** on The project. An older answer shows here only because it already exists.

> 💡 **Tip:** The brief saves by itself about a second after each change. You can close the lead and come back: it reopens where you stopped.

## 6. What happens next
- **The client's story and budget** are saved on the lead itself. Sales shows the same fields, and they stay on the lead after the job is created.
- **The design team** is saved to the client's contacts and linked to this lead (and its job). The architect's brief is addressed to the named architect; until one is named it says "The design team".
- **Rooms and answers** are saved on the lead until a project record exists. When the job is created they move onto the project record once, by themselves, and a message says so. Nothing is sent to the client.
- **The architect's brief** (Documents, see 21-03) reads all of this:
  - each room prints its one Work word and its room brief. A room with work and no brief prints "Brief to follow." in a draft and stops the brief being issued;
  - the budget band prints only when **Confirmed with the client** is ticked;
  - a choice the client has approved that still needs a consultant to check it prints once, with "(agreed with the client; for consultant review)";
  - Blue Leaf's own follow-ups don't print;
  - the client, site and project lines can't be edited in Documents. Change them in Sales (**Change in Sales ›**).
- **Actions:** every open point goes on the Actions list (the pill in the header), under **No one named yet** until someone is named. The points for the next drawing issue print in the architect's brief.
- Nothing in this SOP sends an email, a notification or a portal invitation. Every selection stays internal until staff issue it (see 21-02).

## 7. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it happens | How to avoid it |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Re-typing the address, client or project type into the brief | Habit from paper briefs | They come from Sales. Tap **Change in Sales ›** and fix them there |
| Ticking **Confirmed with the client** because the client mentioned a figure | It looks like a formality | Tick it only when the client has confirmed it. Until then the brief says "To be confirmed by Blue Leaf", which is correct |
| Typing a budget into the story boxes | Old habit | Use the budget bands. They are the only place a dollar figure is allowed in the architect's brief |
| Typing "New kitchen" into the room brief | Habit from the old scope questions | The Work word says what happens to the room. The room brief says what the room must do |
| Leaving a WC or robe with **Brief needed** | It seems too small to brief | Tap **Nothing special — standard room**. Rooms with work and no brief stop the architect's brief being issued |
| Moving rooms upstairs one at a time | Not knowing about tick mode | Tap **Choose upstairs rooms**, tick them all, and move them in one go |
| Tapping **Change to New** when the room really is a renovation | Reading the banner quickly | The banner keeps only one answer. **Keep (Work word)** keeps what the room is set to. Use **Undo** straight away if you tapped the wrong one |
| Changing a red or amber house line "just for this room" in its side panel | It opens from the room | That panel changes the choice for the whole house. Use **Different here** for this room only |
| Treating **Confirm all** as the client's sign-off | The tick looks final | It only says the house choices apply here. Approval is recorded in **Product details…** with who, when and how |
| Putting a supplier price in a note | Old habit | Prices never go in the brief. The estimator links the estimate line in the office Register |
| Typing the architect's name in a note | Old habit | Use **Name the architect**. A note never reaches the brief's "Prepared for" |

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem the user sees | Most likely cause | Fix |
|----------------------|-------------------|-----|
| **Not saved — retrying** in the header, or **Not saved — will retry** in the room bar | No connection, or the save was refused | Keep working; it retries by itself. Check the message at the top of the brief if it doesn't clear |
| **Still not saved. Stay here or carry on…** after tapping Next | The phone has no signal | Tap **Stay** or **Carry on**. Your answers are kept on the phone and saved when the signal is back |
| **Unsaved changes from … are kept on this device** | The page closed before the last change saved | Tap **Restore** to save them, or **Discard** if you meant to drop them |
| A notice says someone else changed an item | Two people edited the same thing | Both changes are kept where they can be; where they clash, the newer copy is kept and you're told which item to re-check |
| **Not saved — try again** under a story box | The client's details couldn't be saved | Tap **try again**. Your words stay in the box |
| The gaps strip still lists "budget" after you chose a band | The save hadn't finished | Wait for **Saved ✓**. The strip updates when the client's details reload |
| The brief still says the budget is to be confirmed | **Confirmed with the client** isn't ticked, or Sales holds words such as "Pending bank approval" | Tick it once the client confirms. Words in Sales that aren't a plain yes don't count |
| **Re-check** says it couldn't reach the mapping service | The mapping service is down | Nothing changed. Try again in a minute |
| **Re-check** says "Couldn't find the site address on the map. Check the address in Sales." | The site address or suburb in Sales isn't one the map can find | Nothing changed. Correct the address or suburb in Sales, then tap **Re-check**: it runs straight away once the address is corrected. Re-typing the same address waits a minute |
| The architect is named but the brief says "The design team" | The person was typed in Sales, not named in the design team | Tap **Name the architect** and pick them. Only a design-team contact prints |
| "Someone else uses that email" | That email belongs to another contact | Tap **Use (their name)** if it is the same person, or **Back** and change the email |
| No **+ Add room here** on the floor header | The job has only one floor | Use **+ Add room** at the top. Each floor gets its own once there are two floors |
| The room chips in the add panel are greyed | No Work word chosen yet | Tap **New**, **Renovated**, **Altered** or **No change** first |
| A room shows **Check work** | An earlier answer about the whole room disagrees with its Work word | Open the room and tap **Keep (Work word)** or **Change to (word)** |
| A room shows **Unfinished: “…”** | The room brief ends in a dash or colon | Open the room and finish the sentence |
| **Can't remove: it holds items on the client portal or in the contract** | Something in the room was issued or signed | Use **Not in this job** instead |
| A room asks no choices and says "Nothing else to choose here first. More is below." | The room type has nothing to ask first | Tap **More choices**, or set the brief and move on |
| Choices for the wrong room type | The wrong room type was picked | On the Rooms list, tap the room's ⋯ → **Room type and drawing sheet…** and choose the right type. Nothing recorded is lost |
| No existing-building questions on a renovation | The project type isn't Extension or Renovation | Header ⋯ → tick **Ask about the existing building**, or fix the project type in Sales |
| No **Gutters** row | Gutters appear under a roof once it has an answer | Answer the roof first |
| "This room is part of the signed contract — change it through a variation." | The contract has been signed | Raise a variation. The brief can't change it |
| No **Project brief** card; the old Finishes schedule shows instead | The register isn't switched on for this lead | Ask the director to add the lead to the register pilot. What was typed in the old list is kept |

## 11. Automation notes
- **Story and budget** (StoryFields): each field writes its lead column through `patchLead` → `PATCH /api/sales/leads/:id`, then the register context reloads. Columns: vision `wo_client_vision`, must-haves `key_requirements`, worries `wo_biggest_concern`, excited `wo_most_excited_about`, budget `construction_budget_min` / `construction_budget_max` (numbers, ex GST; band values from `BUDGET_BANDS`), confirmed `wo_budget_confirmed` (the toggle writes `"Yes"`, off writes null), paying by `finance_status` (`cash` · `needs_finance` · `preapproved` · `unsure`), target start `desired_start_date` (`YYYY-MM-01`). Text saves 1.2 s after the last keystroke or on blur. The server checks the body with `normaliseStoryPatch` (unknown `finance_status`, a non-number budget, lower > upper and a bad date are refused with "Nothing was saved.") and `budgetPairError` when only one figure is sent. The register keeps no copy (Canonical Data Law).
- **Confirmed** is read by `budgetConfirmedOn`: boolean true, or text starting with "yes", "y", "true" or "confirmed". Any other words ("Pending bank approval") read as not confirmed and show as `confirmedNote`. `budgetLine` prints the band only when it is confirmed (owner decision 1; `BUDGET_BAND_RX` is the one line allowed to hold dollars).
- **Gaps strip:** `projectGaps(register, context)` uses the same `contentChecks("architect_brief", …)` as Documents (`story`, `architect`), plus `meta.planRev` (the release blocker `drawing_basis`).
- **Top priorities:** the `P-005` record (no room): chips write `answer.selected` (max 3); "Noted earlier" is its `description`; **Use these** is `prioritiesFromNote`.
- **Delivery model:** `PUT /api/register/leads/:leadId/facts` `{ deliveryModel }` (`leads.delivery_model` before a job, the facts service after). **Project type:** `patchLead({ project_type })` before a job, `PUT …/facts { projectType }` after.
- **Site and planning:** read from `site_council`, `site_zone`, `site_bushfire_prone`, `site_slope_band`, `site_complexity` on the job, else the lead (`registerContext.siteFacts`). Display only. **Re-check** → `POST /api/register/leads/:leadId/site/recheck`: skipped under 10 minutes or while running (`recheckSkipWords`), a failed lookup retries after 1 minute (429), and a failed service never wipes stored values (`recheckFailureWords`).
- **During the works:** `meta.occupancy` (`vacant` · `partly` · `occupied`). The older `W-007` / `P-008` records show as "Earlier note" (`HIDDEN_SITE_PROMPTS`). **Tidy up**, **Edit** and the Access link open the record in `EditDrawer` ("Edit what prints").
- **Design team:** search `GET /api/sales/contacts/search?q=`; save `POST /api/sales/leads/:id/design-team` `{ role, contactId | name, company, email, phone, replaces? }`. It creates `crm_contacts` if needed (`contact_type` by role), upserts a lead-scoped `job_contact_roles` row (`lead_id`, `role`, `status active`), keeps `leads.consultant_roster` in step (`rosterAdd`), and writes `leads.architect_name` (the `architect_name` fact once there is a job). An email used by someone else returns 409 `EMAIL_IN_USE` with a candidate. Remove: `DELETE /api/sales/leads/:id/design-team/:roleId?contactId=&role=`. Read back through `loadRegisterContext` (`mergeConsultants`, `namedArchitect`).
- **Drawings:** `meta.planRev`; the file count is `context.drawings`.
- **Header ⋯:** `meta.renovationOverlay` (disabled when `isRenovationProject(projectType)`).
- **Rooms:** `meta.rooms[]` (`id`, `name`, `level`, `roomType`, `scope`, `order`, `sheet`, `defaultConfirmations`). Every list change is one `replaceItems` write built from the current list (`roomScreen.js`: `addRoomMeta`, `duplicateRoomMeta`, `moveRoomStep`, `dropRoomBefore`, `tickMoveMeta` / `restoreLevels`, `setRoomScope`, `renameRoomMeta`, `removeRoomPlan`). Floors added with no room yet live in `meta.floors` until a room is on them (`floorsPatch`). Floor names go through `normaliseFloor`. Remove deletes the room's records; it is refused (`removalBlocked`) when any record is `sync.baselined`, `sync.locked` or `sync.clientVisible`.
- **Work word:** `room.scope` = `new` · `renovated` · `altered` · `existing_remain` (No change) · `not_in_scope` (Not in this job). Documents read it through `ROOM_SCOPE_DISPOSITION` (renovated/altered → `alter`, no change → `retain`, not in this job → `excluded`).
- **Earlier-answer banner:** `disagreeingScope(register, room)` over the hidden whole-room scope questions (`HIDDEN_ROOM_SCOPE_PROMPTS`: R-003, K-001, B-001, L-001, V-001, D-001, H-001, O-001). Agreement: new ↔ new; renovated/altered ↔ alter; no change ↔ retain; not in this job ↔ excluded; typed words alone never disagree. **Keep** deletes the first disagreeing record (`keepWorkPlan`); **Change to** sets `room.scope` and deletes every record that then disagrees (`changeWorkPlan`). **Undo** calls the store's `undeleteItem` (`registerMerge.planUndelete`): the very record comes back under its own id, where it was in the list. A delete not yet saved is simply dropped (the record keeps its `sync`); one already saved is put back as new under the same id (`sync: null`). Only without `undeleteItem` does it fall back to a copy under a fresh id (`readdIfGone` / `readdable`). A baselined or locked record shows the variation sentence instead (`contractFixed`).
- **Room brief:** one `R-004` record per room, created on the first keystroke or tap from `briefSeed` `{ roomId, promptKey: "R-004", itemKind: "requirement", category: "layout", section: "room" }`. `description` = the sentence; `answer.connectsTo` = room ids or `"outside"`; `answer.usedBy` = `ROOM_USED_BY` keys; `quantity` + `unit: "m²"` = rough size. `answer` stays `null` while only the sentence is written; the first chip, Connects to or Who uses it tap writes an answer with `v: 1` (`toggleAnswerList`), and later taps keep it. **Nothing special** writes `standardBriefText(room)` ("Standard WC; no special requirements.") and counts as a brief (owner decision 3). `isUnfinished` (ends in - – — :) holds a brief back. "Connects to … (set from the …)" is `connectedFrom`, read live.
- **Must-haves:** the room type's layout records (`ROOM_MUST_HAVES`, e.g. kitchen K-002/K-003, WC B-002); a type without one uses R-004's own chips. **Use these** is `usableNotedWords`.
- **Choices:** grouping is `roomLayout()` (primary, **More choices**, **Office only**, house strip, actions, **Other things recorded here**). ⋯ writes: **Not decided yet** `toggleNotDecided` (answer state unresolved), **Not needed here** `toggleNotApplicable`, **Note for the office** `setAnswerNote` (`answer.note`, never printed), **Product details…** PromptRow office mode, **Remove answer** `deleteItem`. Disposition and record type stay in the Register table. `HIDDEN_OPTION_IDS` keeps K-005 engineered stone from ever being a chip. Appliances (`K-008`) are one record per appliance (`answer.element`), supply from **Mostly supplied by**.
- **⚑ Needs action from…:** writes `responsibility`, `actionRole` (only when responsibility is `consultant`), `nextAction`, `actionDue` (`next_issue` · `next_meeting` · `YYYY-MM-DD`) and `resolved`. **+ Needs action from…** creates an `R-010` action record for the room (`actionSeed`). Points with responsibility `blue_leaf` don't print in the architect's brief's 03 list (decision D3). The header pill is `actionsSummary(reg, context)` (next-issue points; `unnamed` drives the amber dot).
- **House strip:** `stripView()`. **Confirm all** writes `room.defaultConfirmations[defaultId] = { at, by, fingerprint, words }` (`confirmRoomDefaults`), nothing else. **Different here** creates a room record with `overridesDefaultId` (`exceptionSeedFrom`, never carrying engineered stone). Red and amber lines are `houseLinesFor()`; **Change** / **Open** edit the house record itself (`isDefault`).
- **Throughout / Site & existing:** `sectionLayout(register, context, section)`; the same `ChoiceRow` as rooms. `P-006` (Areas in the project) is on Site with disposition as the answer (`included` · `undecided` (Optional) · `excluded` · `deferred`). Existing works = `W-004` with `W-006`, `W-008` and the rooms' `R-013`. Gutters are linked rows (`answer.linkedItemIds`). Pricing guide = `meta.baseline`.
- **Saving:** `useRegister` autosaves 1.2 s after a change, retries every 5 s when offline, checks versions and merges per item. Header words: "Saving…", "Saved h:mm pm", `SAVE_RETRY_WORDS` ("Not saved — retrying"). Room bar: `roomSaveState` + `saveWords` ("Not saved — will retry"). **Next** calls `flushWithin(flush, { ms: 8000 })`. Unsaved edits are kept per staff member on the device (draft recovery, 7 days, cleared on sign-out).
- **View rules (nothing stored):** body `data-register-room="open" | "tick"` hides `[data-stage-bar]` on a phone; `data-register-open` makes the lead header and project bar static on capture tabs. Phone layout below 640 px (`useNarrow`); **Expand** from 768 px.
- **Per-viewer storage** (wrapped in try/catch): `register.open.<leadId>`, `register.rooms.filter.<leadId>`, `register.rooms.notNow.<leadId>` (localStorage); `register.rooms.addWork` and `register.room.prevScope.<roomId>` (session).
- **Where it is stored:** before a project exists, `leads.selections_schedule` (lead mode). After the job is created, one automatic promotion moves it to `client_selections` (staged `client_visible = false`), `room_scope_items` and `projects.register_meta`, with a `job_events` row `register.promoted`; the lead copy then refuses writes (409 `REGISTER_PROMOTED`).
- **Feature switch:** `REGISTER_ENABLED=true` or `REGISTER_PILOT_LEADS=<lead id>,…`. Off: every `/api/register/leads/:id` route returns 404 `REGISTER_DISABLED` and the stage shows the pre-register lists.
- **Access:** every route uses `requireAuth`, which returns 403 `Forbidden` for a portal client.
- No email, notification or portal invitation is sent by anything in this SOP.

## 12. Edge cases and limits
- **Two people on one lead:** changes to different items merge. The same field changed by both shows a notice naming what to re-check ("Changed elsewhere — check").
- **The card on two layouts:** the lead page can hold the card twice (desktop and phone layouts). Only the one on screen loads and saves.
- **Floors:** "upper", "Upper" and "upper floor" are one floor. Known floors sort in building order (lower ground, ground, upper); others follow in the order added.
- **Numbering:** a new or duplicated room takes the next free number across the whole job ("Bedroom 5", "WC 3").
- **Duplicate** never copies answers, the room brief, the drawing sheet or confirmations.
- **Tick mode** doesn't count a ticked room that's already upstairs. **Undo** lasts about 10 seconds; the add and duplicate messages last about 6 seconds.
- **The filter and "Not now"** are remembered on this device only. Another device still shows the banner.
- **Top priorities:** at most three. A fourth chip is greyed until one is taken off.
- **Budget:** a typed range that equals a band shows as that band. A lower figure above the upper one is refused. Tapping a chosen band clears both figures.
- **Target start** offers this year and the next four (plus any year already stored).
- **Re-check** runs at most once every 10 minutes per lead; after a failure, wait a minute.
- **Engineered stone** is never offered as a choice. An older answer holding it shows as not allowed and stops the client documents being issued until it is changed.
- **After the contract:** the room brief, its chips and size are read-only ("Part of the signed contract — change it through a variation."), the earlier-answer banner sends you to a variation, and a contract-locked choice says "Approved under the contract — change it through a variation." The ⚑ on such a record still opens, but read-only, with "Locked by the contract — raise a variation." (see 21-05). A record the contract doesn't fix keeps a working ⚑.
- **Nothing is hidden:** a question that isn't asked any more still shows its answer, either in the earlier-answer banner or under **Other things recorded here**.
- **Old typed answers** stay as typed and show as "Noted earlier: …". **Use these** only ticks chips whose names match exactly.
- **Not in this job** on the room screen remembers the room's last Work word for this browser tab, so **Bring it back** returns it. In a new tab it asks "Bring it back as" with the four Work words.
- **Expand** is offered from 768 px wide and closes by itself if the window gets narrower. **Change in Sales ›** and **Upload in Files ›** also leave it, so you can see Sales.
- **Feature switch off:** the card is hidden and the stage shows the old lists exactly as before.
