# SOP: Capture the project brief — Project, Whole house, Site & existing, Rooms

**Module:** Sales → Lead → Project brief & register
**SOP ID:** 21-01
**Status:** Draft
**Priority:** High

---

## 1. Who uses this
Director, sales lead, estimator, project manager — anyone meeting the client or walking the site.

## 2. When to use it
At a Discovery meeting, a concept meeting, a site walk, or any time the client tells you what they want. Detailed room work normally starts once the PTSA is signed, but you can work ahead at any stage — nothing waits for a payment.

## 3. What this does
It records what the client wants, room by room, in one place. Each answer becomes one row in the project register: a **scope** item (what work happens), a **requirement** (what the client needs it to do), a **selection** (a product or finish) or an **action** (something someone must check). Facts Sales already holds — client, site, budget, project type, consultants, meetings — are shown, never typed again. The same rows later produce the client brief, the architect brief and the selections schedule.

## 4. Before you start
- The lead exists and has a project type (Sales → lead → Key details).
- You are signed in as staff (Admin). Portal clients never see this screen.
- Know the current drawing set and revision, if there is one (for example "Concept set B — 03 Nov").
- The register is switched on for this lead (pilot). If it is off, the stage shows the old **Finishes schedule** / **Provisional fittings & fixtures schedule** lists instead — ask the director to add the lead to the pilot.

## 5. Step-by-step process

1. Go to **Sales → Pipeline** and open the lead.
2. In the current stage panel, find the card **Project brief & register**. Click the header to expand it if it is collapsed.
3. **Project** step:
   1. Check **On file in Sales — not re-entered here**. If something is wrong, fix it in the lead's own details or Sales stage, not here.
   2. Under **Project setup**, enter the **Current drawing set / revision (P-002)**, choose the **Delivery model (P-007)** (Design & build, Build only, Construction management or Other) and **Occupancy during works (P-008)**.
   3. Turn on **Existing-building prompts** for any renovation or extension.
   4. Under **Rooms & levels — follow the plans**, tap **+ Kitchen**, **+ Bathroom** and so on (or **+ Other**) for each room on the plan, level by level. Rename a room by typing over its name.
   5. Tap the client's top priorities (P-005, up to three) and add the areas of work (P-006). The box under the priorities is a **note for the client brief**: the client reads it, so write it for them. Use **+ Note** for anything staff-only.
4. **Whole house** step: set each standard once for the whole home. Tap, don't type.
   1. Tap the choice the client describes, for example **Render**. Only the choices that follow from it appear next, for example **Rendered AAC**. Stop whenever the conversation stops: you don't need an exact product.
   2. A tick and the line **Recorded:** show what is held. The card then says **Saving…** and **Saved**. That word is about this card only. If a change on another item is refused, only that card says **Not saved**.
   3. To change your mind, tap the choice again, or tap a different one. If an earlier answer changes and something chosen under it no longer fits, a yellow box lists it. Tap **Use again** or **Remove**. Words typed on an **Other** area or element stay in that box too if you tap a different one, with **Remove** only. Nothing disappears by itself.
   4. No answer yet? Tap **Not decided**. You can add why (**Client unsure**, **Decide later**, **Blue Leaf to advise**, **Designer / consultant to confirm**, **Awaiting drawings / info**) and who has it. Neither is required, and no note is required.
   5. Tap **N/A** if the question doesn't apply. On product and finish choices you can also tap **By others** or **Excluded**. N/A doesn't clear a technical check: an item marked **Needs verification** stays on the outstanding list until someone changes it in **Office fields**.
   6. **Other** opens a small box for the client's own words. **+ Note** adds a short staff-only note. **+ More detail** shows the rarer options, **Deferred**, **Existing to remain**, and finish and product boxes. Once set, **Deferred** or **Existing to remain** stays lit on the card, and a finish or product has its own line, so you can see them next meeting without opening anything.
   7. Where a question can have more than one answer on its own line (several wall systems, several roofs, inclusions), tap **+ Add another**. Each row is saved once you answer it and belongs to the room you are in. Tap **×** to remove a row.
   8. Where each zone is its own standard (floors, paint, kitchen, wet areas, laundry), every usual zone already has a row, so you tap the choice and nothing else. Rarer zones are one tap away: **+ Scullery**, **+ General joinery**, **+ Ensuite**, **+ Powder / WC**, **+ Mudroom**, **+ Other zone**. A zone already answered can't be answered twice.
   9. **Roof:** choose **Applies to** first if there is more than one roof, then material, pitch, profile and colour tone. Once a roof is on the record a **Gutters** row appears under it. Gutters are their own record and don't go in the roof's colour.
   10. **Paint and ceilings:** one row per surface. Each surface says its usual finish, for example **Low sheen** for walls. You don't need to touch it. Tap **+ More detail** on that row only if this client wants a different finish level.
   11. **Doors:** door height, jamb or edge detail and hardware come first. Door type, skirting, architraves and cornice are under **+ More detail**. Fire, acoustic or height exceptions are recorded on the room (R-007).
   12. **Envelope quality:** tap the goals the client cares about. A yellow **For technical review** box lists what those goals may prompt Blue Leaf or a consultant to look at. It is staff-only and marked **Review required**. Nothing in it is selected, specified or priced, and a **Passive House target** doesn't mean the home will be certified. When a review settles something, record it on the card it belongs to.
   13. Questions about **who supplies** (appliances, tapware, fixtures and the like): tapping **Client**, **Blue Leaf**, **Trade** or **Others** records who supplies and nothing else. It doesn't say whether the work is in the contract, so a status already set stays as it was, and an item with no status stays on the outstanding list until someone sets **Scope / disposition** in **Office fields**.
   14. Choosing something records a preference only. To record a client approval, or a technical check, open **Office fields** on that item. Those need their evidence, as before.
   15. **Blue Leaf pricing guide**, at the end of Whole House, after the client's direction. It is for the estimator and staff only. Tap **Standard**, **Upgraded** or **Premium** as the project guide. That moves nothing by itself. Open **By category** to set a tier for each category, or tap **Use … for the N categories with nothing set**. A row with nothing chosen then shows **Blue Leaf pricing assumption**. Once the client chooses, the choice takes over and the row stops showing it. Changing a tier never changes what the client chose, never counts as a preference or an approval, and is not a price.
5. **Site & existing** step: answer access, occupancy, existing-building condition and unknowns for the whole project (not per room). Answers here are recorded as what they are. A steep driveway is a site fact, not building work.
6. **Rooms** step:
   1. Pick a room from the room list at the top (or step with ← and →; it shows "Room 4 of 8"). Check its name, level and type, and set its scope: **New**, **Renovated**, **Existing — Remain** or **Not in scope**. Add the drawing sheet if you are working from one.
   2. At the top of the room, **From Whole House** lists what this room takes from the whole-house standards, one line each: its floor, walls, ceiling, kitchen, wet-area and laundry standards. Tap **Confirm room defaults** to say they apply here. That records applicability only. It is not client approval, a technical check or a price. If one line is different in this room, tap **Different here** on that line and choose. Other rooms don't change. If someone changes a whole-house standard after you confirmed a room, the line says **Changed since confirmed** and nothing moves until you look at it.
   3. Answer the questions listed below that list. Those are the ones this room still has to answer.
   4. On a renovation, **Existing works here** shows the work recorded for this room on Site & Existing. It is the same record, so a change here shows there. **+ Existing work here** adds one.
   3. Tap **Save & next room →** to move on. The last room shows **Save — last room**.
7. Tick **Show detailed prompts** only when you want the detailed (later-stage) questions now.

> 💡 **Tip:** The card saves by itself about a second after you stop typing. Watch the word beside **Save**: *Unsaved… → Saving… → Saved*. You can close the lead and come back — it reopens where you stopped.

## 6. What happens next
- Before a project record exists the brief is saved on the lead ("Lead brief" badge). When the job/project is created, the brief moves onto the project record once, automatically ("Project record" badge). Nothing is sent to the client.
- Every selection stays **internal** (not visible in the client portal) until staff issue it — see 21-02.
- Items that still need a decision, a check or an owner are counted as **outstanding** in the card header.

## 7. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it happens | How to avoid it |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Re-typing the address, budget or consultants into a prompt | Habit from paper briefs | Those come from Sales automatically — fix them at source |
| Ticking **Client-approved** for "they liked it" | Approval means the client has signed off | Use **Preference** or **Proposed**; Client-approved asks for who / when / how |
| Recording the same floor in every room | Not using the whole-house default | Set W-017 once per zone, then Confirm room defaults; use Different here only where a room differs |
| Telling the client "that's Premium, so it costs…" | The pricing guide sits on the page | The guide is an estimating assumption, staff only. It is not a price and not what the client has chosen |
| Reading the review list out as what you will build | It looks like a specification | It is a list of things to check. Nothing on it is selected until someone reviews it and records it on the right card |
| Typing the answer into **+ Note** | Habit from the old text box | Tap the choices. The note is for staff only and doesn't appear in anything the client reads |
| Treating a tick as the client's sign-off | The tick looks final | A tick is a preference. Approval is recorded in **Office fields**, with who, when and how |
| Putting a supplier price in the notes | Old habit | Prices never go in the register — link the estimate line in **Pricing (link — never a price)** |
| Typing "0" for an unmeasured quantity | Field looks required | Leave it blank — blank means *not measured* |

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem the user sees | Most likely cause | Fix |
|----------------------|-------------------|-----|
| "Save failed" beside Save | Lost connection or the server refused the change | Check the message under the header, then tap **Save**. Your typing is kept on screen. |
| A banner says someone else changed an item | Two people edited the same item | The card merges both changes; where both changed the same field it keeps the server's copy and tells you which item to re-check |
| Kitchen prompts appear for a bathroom | Wrong room type picked | Remove the room (✕ **Remove room**) and add it again with the right type |
| No existing-building questions on a renovation | **Existing-building prompts** is off, or the room is set to **New** | Turn the toggle on (Project step) or set the room to **Renovated** |
| A yellow box says **Check these** | An earlier answer changed and something chosen under it no longer fits | Tap **Use again** or **Remove**. It counts as outstanding until you do |
| **Written earlier** under the choices | The item was typed before the cards existed | Your words are kept. Tap the matching choice if you want it structured. The old text stays until you edit it |
| **Earlier work status** on a site question | An older answer used the old dropdown, which didn't make sense there | Tap **Clear** if it's wrong |
| A choice is greyed out | That question has a limit, such as three priorities, or the item is locked | Take one off first. A locked item changes through a variation |
| **Check these** has **Remove** but no **Use again** | The question is already at its limit, such as three priorities | Take one off first, and **Use again** comes back |
| No **Gutters** row | Gutters appear under a roof once the roof has an answer | Answer the roof first |
| **Draft wording** beside the pricing guide | Blue Leaf hasn't confirmed the Standard, Upgraded and Premium wording yet | Treat it as a starting point. It is staff-only either way |
| A room asks a question you thought was set on Whole House | No default for that kind of room, or the default says **Not decided** | Set it on the row for that zone, or answer it in the room |
| "This brief was moved onto the project record" banner | The job/project was created | Normal — keep working; the same rows carry on |
| No **Project brief & register** card; the old Finishes schedule shows instead | The register is switched off for this lead | Ask the director to add the lead to `REGISTER_PILOT_LEADS` (or turn on `REGISTER_ENABLED`) — rows typed in the old list are adopted when it is switched on |

## 11. Automation notes
- Before a project exists: saved to `leads.selections_schedule` (lead scratchpad). Each item is merged per item so two people can work at once.
- When a job is created: one automatic promotion moves the brief into `client_selections` (choices, staged `client_visible = false`), `room_scope_items` (scope, requirement, action) and `projects.register_meta`. A `job_events` row records it. The lead scratchpad then refuses further writes.
- Delivery model before the job: `leads.delivery_model`; after the job: the facts service (`delivery_model` fact), stamped forward at conversion.
- No email, notification or portal invitation is sent by anything in this SOP.

## 12. Edge cases and limits
- Two people on the same lead: changes to different items merge; the same field edited by both shows a conflict notice.
- The card can appear in the desktop and mobile layouts at once; only the visible one loads and saves.
- Question wording comes from the approved Tab 21 library (`tab21-v1.0`); old R02 prompt keys are mapped to the new IDs automatically.
- The choices come from the answer map (`tab21-answer-map-v1.0`). Choices beyond the Tab 21 wording are proposals for Blue Leaf to confirm. A stored answer keeps the words it was recorded with, even if an option is later reworded.
- Older typed answers are never parsed into choices. They stay as they were written.
- Until the document update (R05 P5), a typed answer doesn't appear in the Selections Schedule or most of the Client and Architect Briefs. Priorities do.
- If the database is missing migration 213, the card still saves to the lead, and document issuing is switched off with a notice.
- Feature switch (server): `REGISTER_ENABLED=true` (every lead) or `REGISTER_PILOT_LEADS=<lead id>,…` (pilot leads only). Default off: the card is hidden, every `/api/register` route returns 404 `REGISTER_DISABLED`, no automatic promotion runs, and the stage shows the pre-register lists exactly as before.
