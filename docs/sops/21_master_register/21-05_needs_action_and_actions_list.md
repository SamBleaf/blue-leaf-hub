# SOP: Needs action and the Actions list — ask someone, set a by-when, close it

**Module:** Sales → Lead → Project brief → the flag (⚑) on every row, and **Actions**
**SOP ID:** 21-05
**Status:** Draft
**Priority:** High

---

## 1. Who uses this
Director, project manager, estimator, sales lead and office staff. Anyone who takes the brief at a meeting or on site, or follows up with the client, the architect and the consultants.

## 2. When to use it
- Something in the brief needs someone to do something: the client to decide, the architect to draw it, the engineer or energy assessor to check it, a supplier to confirm it, or Blue Leaf to find out.
- You want to see everything still waiting on someone, grouped by who.
- You are about to send the architect's brief and want each "needed for the next drawing issue" point to name who, and by when.
- A drawing, an engineer's letter or a supplier's sheet has answered a point and you want to close it.

## 3. What this does
Every row in the brief has a small flag (⚑). Two taps on it say who needs to act (the words and the by-when fill in for you). The Actions list, opened from the **Actions** button at the top, shows everything waiting on someone, grouped by who, in the same order the architect reads it. What you set there is exactly what prints in the architect's brief under **Needed for the next drawing issue** (section 03) and, for the client, in the client's brief under **Decisions to make**. Blue Leaf's own follow-ups stay on our list and never print in the architect's brief. Nothing is ever sent from here: you send the documents yourself.

## 4. Before you start
- You are signed in as staff. Portal clients never see these screens.
- The lead's brief is switched on and open: in the lead's stage panel, the **Project brief** card shows the tabs **The project · Rooms · Throughout · Site & existing · Documents**. If it is collapsed, tap **Continue brief →** (or **Start brief →**).
- To have the architect's firm named on the Who choices and in the brief, name the architect first: **The project** → **Design team** → **Name the architect**. Engineers and energy assessors added to the design team are named the same way.
- A record that is part of a signed contract can't be changed here (see 21-04).

## 5. Step-by-step process

### 5.1 Read the flag on a row

The flag sits on every record row: room choices, the "Same as the rest of the house" strip in a room, the rows on **Throughout** and **Site & existing**, the office **Register** table, and the **Edit what prints** drawer.

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Grey outline flag | Nothing is needed. Tap it to ask someone anyway. |
| Amber outline flag with a small dot | Open, but no one has been asked yet: a check still to be done, a choice not decided, or something still "in or out" of the job. |
| A label such as **Architect · Next issue** or **Client · 28 Nov** | Someone has been asked. A red **Overdue** shows on it once a picked date has passed. |
| **✓ Done** (grey) | Closed. It no longer prints, and it is kept. |

Tap any of them to open the **Needs action from…** sheet. On a tablet or computer it opens beside the flag; on a phone it slides up from the bottom.

### 5.2 Ask someone (two taps)

1. Tap the flag on the row (for example **Gutters** on **Throughout**).
2. Read the sheet's title **Needs action from…** and the line under it, which says where the record is and what it is (for example **Throughout · Gutters**).
3. Look at **What's needed**. It is already filled in, for example **Confirm half round**:
   - a record with words or choices reads **Confirm …** (its own words);
   - a choice with nothing chosen reads **Decide the …**;
   - something still in or out of the job reads **Decide whether … is part of this job**;
   - a follow-up reads its own words.
4. Change the words if you need to. Tapping the box selects all of it, so you can type straight over it.
5. Look at **By when**. When a chip is marked **suggested**, it is set for that person: **Next drawing issue** for the architect, engineer, energy assessor or a trade, **Next meeting** for the client or Blue Leaf. When nothing is suggested (for example the washer / dryer layout), nothing is ticked until you tap who.
6. Under **Who**, tap one of the six: **Client · Architect · Engineer · Energy assessor · Blue Leaf · Trade or supplier**. The one marked **suggested** is who the Hub expects (it never suggests the client for a technical check); some rows have no suggestion. Once a firm is on the design team, its name shows under the word (for example **Architect** with **Bright Studio** beneath).
7. A message says, for example, **Architect · next drawing issue — added to Actions**. The row's flag becomes the label **Architect · Next issue**.
8. Read the small note under **What's needed**. It tells you where this will print. Before you tap who it follows the suggested chip, and it is blank when nothing is suggested:
   - Architect, Engineer, Energy assessor, Trade or supplier: **Prints in the architect's brief under “Needed for the next drawing issue”.**
   - Client: **Prints in the client's brief under “Decisions to make”.** For something the design must achieve (a requirement, such as the washer / dryer layout or the heating and cooling) it reads **Prints in the architect's brief under “Needed for the next drawing issue”.** instead, because the client's brief never lists a requirement as a decision (see 5.7).
   - Blue Leaf: **Not in the architect's brief. The client's brief lists it under “Decisions to make”.** For a requirement it reads **Our list only — doesn't print.**
9. The sheet stays open, so you can still change the words or the by-when. Each change saves when you tap it or leave the box.
10. Close the sheet with the **X**, by tapping outside it, or (on a phone) by swiping it down. Nothing you typed is lost.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you clear **What's needed**, the Who choices grey out and the sheet says **Say what's needed first**. Type what you need, then tap who.

> 💡 **Tip:** Naming who on a choice that has nothing chosen yet also marks it as not decided, with the matching reason: Client → deciding later; Architect, Engineer or Energy assessor → designer to confirm; Blue Leaf → Blue Leaf to advise. You don't need to do that separately.

### 5.3 Pick a date

1. Open the sheet from the flag or the label.
2. Under **By when**, tap **Pick a date…**.
3. Choose the date in the calendar that opens.
4. The choice now shows the date with a tick (for example **✓ 28 Nov**), and the row's label reads, for example, **Client · 28 Nov**.
5. If the date is already past, a red **Overdue** shows beside it, on the row's label and in the Actions list. Pick a new date, or close the action.

[insert screenshot: the sheet with a past date picked and the red Overdue tag]

### 5.4 Close it: Done, Checked or Clear

The bottom of the sheet only appears once someone has been asked.

**Done** (anything that isn't a technical check)
1. Open the sheet from the row's label.
2. Tap **Done**.
3. A message says **Marked done.** with **Undo** for 5 seconds. The label becomes **✓ Done**. It leaves the Actions list and stops printing. Its history is kept.

**Checked** (a technical check, for example Gutters, Main walls, Windows)
1. Open the sheet from the row's label.
2. Tap **Checked**. A box **Drawing or document reference** opens.
3. Type the reference that answers it, for example **A301 Rev D detail 4**. **Save** stays grey until you type one: a check can't be closed without a reference.
4. Tap **Save** (or press Enter).
5. A message says **Marked checked.** with **Undo**. The check is recorded as verified, with your name, the time and the reference, and any action on it closes too.

**Clear** (asked the wrong person, or not needed after all)
1. Open the sheet.
2. Tap **Clear** (bottom right). The who, the by-when and the words go. If the question normally belongs to someone (for example heating and cooling belongs to a trade), that stays.
3. The words go back to the suggested wording and the flag goes back to grey or amber.

> 💡 **Tip:** To reopen something marked done, type new words in **What's needed**. Just opening and closing a done one doesn't reopen it.

[insert screenshot: the sheet's bottom row with Checked tapped, the Drawing or document reference box and Save]

### 5.5 The Actions list

1. At the top of the brief, tap **Actions** (for example **Actions 10**). An amber dot on it means some points for the next drawing issue have no one named yet.
2. The list opens on the right (full screen on a phone), titled **Actions · 10**.
3. Choose what to show at the top:
   - **Next drawing issue (10)**: what the architect's next drawing issue needs. This is the list that prints in section 03.
   - **Everything open (12)**: adds the client's finish choices and Blue Leaf's own follow-ups.
4. Read the groups, in the order the architect reads them: **Architect**, **Engineer**, **Energy assessor**, **Consultant**, **Trade or supplier**, **Client**, **Blue Leaf**, then **No one named yet** in amber. Each heading has its count.
5. Read each row: what it is and where (for example **Gutters · Throughout**), then what's needed in black, or an amber prompt (**Nothing asked yet**, **Check still open**, **Not decided**, **In or out of this job?**). A by-when shows on the right once one is set.
6. Read any tags under a row:
   - **Overdue** (red): its date has passed.
   - **Engineered stone can't be used** (red): choose another material.
   - **A technical check — usually not the client's**: ask a trade or the engineer instead.
   - **Say what's needed — won't print until it does**, **Finish the words — won't print until they're finished**, **Its words contradict its status — won't print until fixed**, **Disagrees with the room's Work — won't print**: fix the record, or it won't print.

**Name who in one tap (rows under No one named yet)**
1. Find the row, for example **Gutters**.
2. Tap a name under it: **Architect**, **Engineer**, **Energy assessor** or **Client** (or **More…** for the full sheet).
3. The row fades and moves to that group. A message says, for example, **Architect · next drawing issue — added to Actions**, with **Undo**. Words or a date you had already typed are kept.

**Close a row**
1. Tap the **✓** on the right of the row.
2. For anything that isn't a technical check, the row fades into **Done today** at the bottom, and a message says **Marked done.** with **Undo** for 5 seconds.
3. For a technical check, the **Drawing or document reference** box opens under the row instead. Type the reference and tap **Save** (or **Cancel**).
4. Tap **Done today (n)** to see what you closed while the list was open.

**Open a row**
1. Tap the row's words. The record opens in the **Edit what prints** drawer, with its own flag.
2. A row that says **Nothing asked yet** opens the **Needs action from…** sheet at **What's needed** instead. The first one says so: **Nothing asked yet — tap to say what's needed**.
3. Tapping the by-when on the right, or **More…**, also opens the sheet.

**Copy the list**
1. Tap **Copy list**. It copies the list you are looking at as plain text, one line each, for example:
   `Architect — Windows & glazing priorities (Throughout): confirm double glazed aluminium, Jolong, white frames — next drawing issue`
2. A message says **Copied 10 actions**.
3. Paste it into your own email or notes. Nothing is sent from the Hub.

**Add something for the whole job**
1. Tap **+ Needs action for the whole job** at the bottom.
2. Type what's needed (the box is ready for you).
3. Tap who. It joins that group.

When nothing is open, the list says **Nothing is waiting on anyone.**

### 5.6 Actions in this room

1. Open **Rooms**. A room with open points shows an amber label such as **1 action** on its row (on the sample job, the **Laundry**).
2. Open the room.
3. Scroll to **Actions in this room**. It lists that room's open points, each with its flag and a **✓** (for example **Washer / dryer layout** — **Not decided**).
4. Tap the flag to ask someone, or the **✓** to close it (a technical check asks for its reference first).
5. To add a follow-up for this room, tap **+ Needs action from…**, type what's needed, then tap who.
6. With nothing open, the card says **Nothing waiting on anyone here.**

### 5.7 Where it prints, and what never prints

**Architect's brief, section 03 "Needed for the next drawing issue"**
- Each point is numbered, under the intro "Please reply against these numbers, quoting the drawing number and revision. A point stays open until we've checked the revised drawings against it."
- The table reads **No. · What's needed · Who**. A **By** column is added as soon as any point has a by-when (for example **Next issue**, **Next meeting**, **28 Nov**).
- **Who** names the firm when one is on the design team (for example **Bright Studio**), otherwise the role (**Architect**, **Engineer**, **Energy assessor**, **Client**). A point with no one named prints **To be agreed**.
- Two "in or out" questions in the same place with the same who and by-when are asked in one line, for example **Retaining and pool: are they part of this job?**, even though the Actions list shows them as two rows.
- A point tagged "won't print" waits in the Actions list until you fix it.

**Client's brief, "Decisions to make"**
- The client's own actions and decisions print first, then other open decisions (up to seven in all) that aren't with the architect, engineer or a trade.
- The **Who** column shows who, and the by-when under it: **By our next meeting**, **By the next drawing issue** or **By 28 Nov**. No one named prints **To be agreed**.
- A client point that matters for the drawings also prints in the architect's brief, with **Who: Client**.
- Something the design must achieve (for example the laundry layout, or the heating and cooling) isn't listed in the client's brief, even when the client is asked. It prints in the architect's brief with **Who: Client**, and the sheet's note says so. If the client must answer it, tell them, or add a follow-up for the whole job in your own words.

**What never prints in the architect's brief**
- Blue Leaf's own follow-ups. They are our list only: on the Documents step they are listed under **Not in this brief** → **Kept for the office**.
- The client's finish choices (they go to the client's brief and the selections schedule).
- Anything marked **✓ Done** or checked.

> ⚠️ **Check the client's brief before you send it.** The client's brief lists Blue Leaf's own follow-ups under **Decisions to make**, with **Who: Blue Leaf**, when there is room in its seven (the sheet's note says so: "The client's brief lists it under “Decisions to make”."). Only a Blue Leaf requirement is our list only. Preview the client's brief and reword or close anything the client shouldn't read.

### 5.8 From the Documents step: "Name who"

1. Open **Documents** and tap **Preview** on the **Architect Design Brief**.
2. Under **Before you send**, read the entry such as **5 points for the next drawing issue have no one named: Gutters, Main walls, Washer / dryer layout, Retaining, Pool.**
3. Tap **Name who**. The Actions list opens on **Next drawing issue**, scrolled to **No one named yet**, which is outlined for a moment.
4. Name who on each row (one tap each).
5. Close the list and tap **Preview again**. The entry is gone once every point names someone.

## 6. What happens next
- Each tap saves straight away with the rest of the brief. The row's label, the **Actions** count and the Rooms list update at once.
- The next preview of the architect's brief prints the point in section 03 with its **Who** (and **By**, once any by-when is set). The **Before you send** entry counts the points with no one named.
- The next preview of the client's brief lists the client's actions under **Decisions to make**, with the by-when.
- No email, text, portal message or notification is sent to anyone. You send the documents yourself (21-03), or paste **Copy list** into your own email.
- Done and checked points stay on the record. They just stop showing and printing.

## 7. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it happens | How to avoid it |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Naming the client for a technical check | Habit: the client "owns" the house | Ask the trade, engineer or architect. The list tags it **A technical check — usually not the client's** |
| Expecting Blue Leaf's follow-ups in the architect's brief | They're in the Actions list | They're our list only. Turn them into a point for the architect if the architect needs to act |
| Closing a check with **Done** from memory | Wanting it off the list | Use **Checked** and type the drawing or document reference that answered it |
| Counting 10 actions but seeing 8 numbered points | The list counts each record | Two "in or out" questions print as one line; a point tagged "won't print" waits until fixed |
| Leaving **What's needed** as just the suggestion when more is needed | It is filled in for you | Read it before you tap who; type the real question if it's different |
| Picking a date and forgetting it | Dates aren't reminders | Check the Actions list for red **Overdue** before each meeting |

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem the user sees | Most likely cause | Fix |
|----------------------|-------------------|-----|
| **Say what's needed first** and the Who choices are grey | **What's needed** is empty | Type what's needed, then tap who |
| **Save** under **Drawing or document reference** stays grey | No reference typed | Type the drawing or document reference |
| There's no **Done** or **Checked** in the sheet | No one has been asked yet | Tap who first; the bottom row then appears. Or use the **✓** in the Actions list |
| **Locked by the contract — raise a variation.** and nothing can be changed | The record is in the signed contract or baseline | Raise a variation (see 21-04) |
| A point disappeared from **Next drawing issue** after naming **Blue Leaf** | Blue Leaf's own follow-ups are our list only | Switch to **Everything open**; it is under **Blue Leaf** |
| **Couldn't copy the list on this device.** | The browser blocked copying | Try again in another browser, or select the rows' words by hand |
| No **Actions** button at the top | The brief is collapsed | Tap **Continue brief →** |
| "The project register isn't switched on for this lead." | The lead isn't in the pilot | Ask the director to switch it on |
| The brief's preview still shows the old Who | The preview is older than your change | Tap **Preview again** ("Changed since this preview") |
| A row says **Engineered stone can't be used** | Engineered stone is prohibited | Agree another material with the client (see 21-03) |
| A row says **Say what's needed — won't print until it does** | A follow-up that only names a thing | Write what is needed, for example "Confirm the rangehood position with the joiner" |
| The client's brief lists one of our own follow-ups | Blue Leaf's decisions fill the client's list up to seven (the sheet's note says "The client's brief lists it under “Decisions to make”.") | Reword it for the client, or close it, then preview again |
| The sheet's note under **What's needed** is blank | No one is named yet and the Hub has no suggestion for this row | Tap who: the note then says where it prints |

## 11. Automation notes
- **What a tap writes** (`src/lib/needsAction.js`; the components hold no rules):
  - Who → `item.responsibility` (`client` | `architect` | `consultant` | `blue_leaf` | `trade`). Engineer and Energy assessor are `consultant` with `item.actionRole` `engineer` / `energy`; `actionRole` is dropped for anyone else (`makeItem`).
  - What's needed → `item.nextAction`. By when → `item.actionDue`: `next_issue` | `next_meeting` | `YYYY-MM-DD` | `""` (`normaliseActionDue`).
  - A Who tap (`setActionPatch`) also sets `resolved: false`. An action record with no words takes the typed words as `description`.
  - Parking (`parkPatch`): a guided choice with no answer gets `answer.state: "unresolved"` and `answer.unresolved.reason` (`PARK_REASON`: client → `decide_later`; architect, engineer, energy → `designer_to_confirm`; blue_leaf → `blue_leaf_to_advise`; trade → no reason). Never on `certainty: "client_approved"`, `sync.locked`, `sync.baselined`, an in/out element or an action. A reason that names no one (`awaiting_info`) is kept. `answer.v` and other answer keys are kept (`itemPatch`).
  - Inline chips in the Actions list use `assignPatch`: typed `nextAction` and `actionDue` are kept; a blank record takes `prefillWhat` and `defaultDue`.
  - Closing the sheet saves only words really typed (`whatToSaveOnClose`).
  - Done → `{ resolved: true }`. Checked → `{ technicalStatus: "verified", technicalEvidence: { at, by, ref } }` (`by` = signed-in staff `full_name`, else email, else "Blue Leaf"), plus `resolved: true` when it carried an action, a by-when or words; `null` (nothing saved) without a reference. Clear → `{ responsibility: question.owner || "", actionRole: "", nextAction: "", actionDue: "", resolved: false }`. Undo → `undoPatchFor` puts back exactly the fields the patch changed.
  - New follow-ups: whole job → `W-025` action (`WHOLE_JOB_ACTION_SEED`, section `project`, category `notes`); a room → `R-010` action (`actionSeed` in `src/lib/roomScreen.js`).
- **Saving:** through the register store (`useRegister`) → `PUT /api/register/leads/:leadId` `{ meta?, changes[] }`. Lead mode: `leads.selections_schedule`. Project mode: `register_detail` (`detailOf`), with an append-only `job_events` history row per change. `actionRole` / `actionDue` ride `ITEM_FIELDS` in the three-way merge (`registerMerge.js`); the server keeps a stored value when an incoming item lacks the key. No migration.
- **What is open, and the order:** `openPoints(register, context, { scope, merge })` in `src/lib/briefModel.js`. Open = not resolved AND (nextAction, an action record, `technicalStatus: "needs_verification"`, `answer.state: "unresolved"`, or an in/out element); whole-room scope answers never. `forNextIssue` keeps actions, the design team's (`architect`, `consultant`, `trade`), technical checks, `DRAWING_IMPACT` questions, requirements and in/out elements, and always leaves out `blue_leaf` (owner decision D3). Group order `WHO_ORDER`: architect, engineer, energy, consultant, trade, client, blue_leaf, none.
- **Actions list / header pill:** `actionPoints` = `openPoints` with `merge: false` (each record its own row). Header pill = `actionsSummary` → `{ count, unnamed }` (`RegisterWorkshop.jsx`, `data-testid="actions-pill"`). "Done today" is panel state only, cleared when the list closes. Copy list = `copyListText` via `navigator.clipboard.writeText`, falling back to a hidden textarea and `execCommand("copy")`.
- **Architect's brief 03:** `architectBriefModel().points` (merged, held points removed) → `buildArchitectBriefPayload` sets `HAS_BY` / `NO_BY`; template `scripts/documents/author-adb.mjs` prints "No. | What's needed | Who" plus "By" when `HAS_BY`. Who = `whoOf().label` (named firm, else role, else "To be agreed"). Not-printed groups: `office` (Blue Leaf's open points), `client`, `schedule`, `held`, `empty`, `waiting`.
- **Client's brief "Decisions to make":** `clientBriefFixture` in `server/lib/registerDocuments.mjs`: open items with an action or decision, `itemKind !== "requirement"`, responsibility not `architect` / `consultant` / `trade`; every `client` one, then others to seven. Owner = `responsibilityLabel`, by = `byWords(actionDue, "client")`.
- **The sheet's footnote:** `footnoteFor(item, currentKey || suggested)` asks the same rules the briefs use: design team → `FOOTNOTE_ARCHITECT`; client → `FOOTNOTE_CLIENT`, except a requirement that `forNextIssue` keeps → `FOOTNOTE_ARCHITECT`; Blue Leaf → `FOOTNOTE_BLUE_LEAF` ("Not in the architect's brief. The client's brief lists it under “Decisions to make”."), except a requirement → `FOOTNOTE_OURS` ("Our list only — doesn't print."); no one and no suggestion → no footnote. Tested in `register-needs-action` ("footnote by who …").
- **Content check:** `contentChecks` key `unnamed` (`src/lib/registerQuality.js`, `merge: false`, held points excluded) → Fix "Name who" → `ActionsPanel` `initialFilter: "unnamed"`.
- **Rooms:** `roomActionPoints` (scope `all`, the room's own records) feeds **Actions in this room** and the Rooms list label (`actionsPillText`).
- No email, SMS, portal invitation, notification or outbound request of any kind.

## 12. Edge cases and limits
- **Two taps:** the flag, then who. Changing the by-when is one more tap. Marking done is one tap in the Actions list, or two from a row.
- **Before anyone is named:** words typed and the sheet closed wait under **No one named yet**.
- **By-when before who:** a by-when you pick before tapping who is kept. Otherwise tapping who sets that person's default.
- **Overdue** is judged on the device's own date. A date equal to today isn't overdue. **Next drawing issue** and **Next meeting** never go overdue.
- **In or out pairs** (for example Retaining and Pool) are two rows in the list and one printed line, while they share the same who and by-when. Give them different people and they print separately.
- **Held points** (tagged "won't print") count in the list and the **Actions** number, never in the brief.
- **Blue Leaf:** naming Blue Leaf moves a point off **Next drawing issue** (the **Actions** number drops by one) and under **Blue Leaf** in **Everything open**.
- **Client-approved records** keep their approval: asking someone, Done, Checked, Clear and Undo never change what the client approved.
- **Locked records** open read-only, with **Locked by the contract — raise a variation.** The Actions list shows the same words and no **✓** or one-tap names on them.
- **A record not answered yet:** the flag still works on the row. The first Who tap creates the record, and a quick second tap can't make a second one.
- **Done today** shows only what was closed while the list was open. Closing the list clears it (the records stay closed).
- **Undo** lasts 5 seconds. Messages without Undo last about 3 seconds.
- **Two people at once:** the brief's usual save and conflict rules apply (see 21-01).

## 14. Troubleshoot Agent Test Script

> **For the troubleshoot agent only.** Run against the loopback register sandbox (`scripts/dev/register-sandbox`, SPA `http://localhost:5184`, API `http://localhost:8797`), never production. Don't run e2e, playwright or batch suites that target production. Record pass/fail against each item.

### Pre-test setup
- [ ] Sandbox running (`postgrest`, `gateway`, `api`, `vite`) with the register switched on for test leads (`REGISTER_ENABLED=true` in the sandbox env). LibreOffice is installed for the PDF previews.
- [ ] Signed in as the sandbox staff user (`~/.blh-register-sandbox/credentials.local.json`). Browser devtools open on Console and Network.
- [ ] **L1, load the real job onto a fresh test lead** (repeat this for each test case that says "fresh L1"):
  1. `POST /api/sales/leads` with `site_address: buildTestJobAddress({ suite: "REGISTER-ACTIONS", workflowId: "21-05" })` (`scripts/lib/testArtifactPrefixes.mjs`), `project_type: "extension"`, `stage: "consultants"`, `suburb: "Seaview Park"`, `lead_source: "referral"`, `is_test: true` (without a lead source the create is refused: "lead_source_category is required (or a lead_source we can classify)."). If the lead comes back at another stage, set `stage` with the service client as `seed-staging.mjs` does.
  2. Take `realJob()` from `scripts/documents/fixtures/real-extension-job.mjs`. Delete each item's `sync` (a fresh lead has no stored versions, so the server would answer 409 "missing").
  3. `PUT /api/register/leads/:id` with `{ meta: { ...register.meta, rev: 0 }, changes: register.items }`. Expected: `ok: true`. (The design-team, site, finance and start-date facts stay empty on L1, so expectations that need them, such as the 37 printed lines, are checked in TC-16, not on screen.)
  4. Add no design team (no architect, engineer or energy assessor).
- [ ] Note today's date in Adelaide (the device clock decides Overdue).
- [ ] Starting state on L1: header **Actions 10** with an amber dot; Actions list **Next drawing issue (10)** | **Everything open (12)**; groups **Architect · 2**, **Energy assessor · 1**, **Client · 2**, **No one named yet · 5** (i27 Gutters, i47 Main walls, i61 Washer / dryer layout, i71 Retaining, i72 Pool).
