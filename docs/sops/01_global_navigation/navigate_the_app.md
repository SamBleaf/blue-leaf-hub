# SOP: Navigate the App

**Module:** Global Navigation  
**SOP ID:** 01-01  
**Status:** Draft  
**Priority:** High

---

## 1. Who uses this
All staff.

## 2. When to use it
Whenever you need to move between different sections of Blue Leaf Hub.

## 3. What this does
Explains the layout of the app and how to get to each section quickly.

## 4. Before you start
- You must be signed in (see SOP 00-01)

## 5. Step-by-step process

### On a desktop or laptop

The app has a **left-hand menu** (sidebar) that is always visible. Click any item to go to that section.

[insert screenshot: Full app with left sidebar visible and menu items labelled]

**Menu items and what they do:**

| Menu item | What it is |
|-----------|-----------|
| Home | Dashboard overview |
| Sales Manager | Lead pipeline and client enquiries |
| Tender Manager | RFQs, fee proposals, cost intelligence |
| Operations | Active projects, schedules, site diary, WHS |
| Finance Manager | Invoices, approvals, job financials |
| Client Portal Admin | Set up and manage client-facing portals |
| Settings | Company details, users, integrations |

[insert screenshot: Left sidebar with each section labelled]

### On a phone or tablet

On mobile, the menu is at the **bottom of the screen** as a tab bar. Tap any icon to switch sections.

[insert screenshot: Mobile bottom navigation bar]

Tap the **≡ More** button to see additional sections not shown in the main tab bar.

### Getting back to a previous page

- Click the **← Back** link or arrow at the top of any detail page to go back one level
- Click the **Home** icon in the menu to return to the main dashboard at any time

[insert screenshot: Back arrow on a detail page]

### Finding your current location

The **page title** at the top of each page tells you where you are. Some pages also show a "breadcrumb trail" (e.g. Operations → 10 Smith Street → Schedule) so you know the path you took.

## 6. What happens next
Once you've navigated to the right section, follow the relevant SOP for the task you're completing.

## 7. Common mistakes
- Looking for a section that isn't in your menu — some sections are only visible to Admins. Check with your admin if you think you're missing access.
- Using the browser's back button instead of the app's back arrow — this sometimes works but can cause pages to reload unexpectedly

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| A menu section is missing | Your role may not have access. Contact your admin. |
| The page is showing an error | Refresh the browser page (press F5). Sign out and sign back in if the error persists. |
| The sidebar is not visible | On desktop, your browser window may be too narrow. Make it wider, or look for a hamburger menu ≡ icon. |
